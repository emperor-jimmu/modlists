# Crafting & Economy

**MO2 Separator:** `Expanded Systems` → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

Part of the [`Expanded Systems`](modlist-expanded-systems.md) section. See also: `Expanded Systems - Character & Progression`, `Expanded Systems - Magic & Perks`, `Expanded Systems - Survival & Needs`, `Expanded Systems - Followers & Reputation`.

## Alchemy Overhaul → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection decides whether alchemy is a disciplined support system for potions, poisons, and ingredients or a much larger rework that also starts reshaping cooking and survival-adjacent loops. The goal is to choose one clear alchemy philosophy before moving into cooking, crafting, and economy decisions that depend on it.

### Baseline

- **Apothecary - An Alchemy Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/52130)) — Main baseline. Balanced modern route. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Alternatives

- **Complete Alchemy and Cooking Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/19924)) — Large integrated comparison if `Elder Wilds` decides alchemy and cooking should become a major combined pillar. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Alchemy Potions and Food Adjustments** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/5877)) — Lighter fallback if the modern overhaul route proves to be more system weight than the list needs. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Notes

- A very large alchemy package can quietly absorb cooking and food design, leaving the next subsection little room to decide anything.
- A restrained alchemy overhaul can undershoot the `Expanded Systems` pillar if too many adjacent systems also land on conservative choices. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- Potion, poison, and ingredient balance can become hard to reason about if the list mixes multiple overlapping consumable philosophies.
- Treat large food-and-cooking coverage as a deliberate expansion choice, not something that should quietly hitchhike into the alchemy baseline.

## Cooking Systems → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection decides whether food and cooking are mostly ambient support for travel and downtime or a more deliberate progression-and-preparation layer in their own right. The goal is to choose a cooking baseline that complements the survival decision without making cooking entirely dependent on a heavy needs framework.

### Baseline

- **Gourmet - A Cooking Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/96876)) — Main baseline. Balanced modern route. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Alternatives

- **Complete Alchemy and Cooking Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/19924)) — Integrated comparison if the list later decides alchemy and cooking should be tightly fused. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Alchemy Potions and Food Adjustments** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/5877)) — Lighter fallback if the modern cooking route turns out to be more system weight than needed. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Mealtime - A Food and Recipe Mod** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/22496)) — Recipe-density companion if `Gourmet`'s recipe scope feels too narrow after playtesting. Adds new foods and recipes while sticking close to vanilla ingredient logic. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Visual Companions

- **Hyper Realistic Cooked Beef** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/173752)) and **Hyper Realistic Bread** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/173283)) — Mesh/texture-only replacers for cooked-beef and bread models. Zero plugin overhead. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Skyrim 3D Cooking** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/23007)) — Replaces cooking pot, oven, and food-prep meshes with higher-detail 3D versions. Mesh-only, zero plugin overhead. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Realistic HD Food Remastered** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/22087)) — Replaces vanilla food textures with higher-resolution, photorealistic versions covering bread, meat, fruits, vegetables, and prepared dishes. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Notes

- Cooking can become too dependent on the survival framework and stop making sense as its own system.
- A very large cooking package can quietly absorb alchemy and economy ownership.
- A very light cooking layer can make inns, food, and downtime loops too forgettable to justify separate attention.
- Judge cooking on inns, camps, home bases, travel prep, and downtime rhythm rather than on recipe volume alone.

## Smithing And Crafting Expansion → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection decides what crafting is supposed to do in `Elder Wilds`: lightly improve the vanilla smithing loop, become a broader rules-and-materials framework, or shift more responsibility toward NPC services and roleplay texture.

### Baseline

- **Ars Metallica - Smithing Enhancement** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/321)) — Player-crafting baseline. Handles smithing XP, tanning, mining, and basic crafting-loop improvements without heavy rules restructuring. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Honed Metal - NPC Crafting and Enchanting Services** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/61015)) — Locked NPC-service companion. Lets the player pay smiths and enchanters to craft, temper, enchant, and recharge items, making town services and gold economy matter without replacing the player crafting loop. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Thaumaturgy - An Enchanting Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/57138)) — Simonrim-aligned enchanting overhaul that rebalances enchantment effects, costs, and progression. Designed to pair with `Mysticism` and `Adamant` baselines, closing the Simonrim crafting loop. Prerequisite for artifact-overhaul content. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Alternatives

- **Complete Crafting Overhaul Remastered** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/28608)) — High-complexity comparison if `Elder Wilds` decides material logic and crafting rules should become a major progression pillar. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Summermyst - Enchantments of Skyrim** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/6285)) — Optional enchantment variety companion adding ~140 new enchantment effects to loot. Sits alongside `Thaumaturgy` rather than replacing it. Needs compatibility verification against `Thaumaturgy`, `Adamant`, `Mysticism`, and the chosen artifact overhaul before locking. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Notes

- A very large crafting framework can quietly absorb economy and loot-balance ownership before those sections have a chance to make their own decisions.
- A very light crafting route can leave smithing too forgettable to justify separate attention within `Expanded Systems`. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- Mixing player-centric crafting expansion with strong NPC-service assumptions can produce an incoherent progression loop.
- `Ars Metallica` and `Honed Metal` serve different purposes and should be locked together as complementary rather than competing.

## Economy And Loot Balance → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection decides what economy and loot are supposed to feel like in `Elder Wilds`: a lightly rebalanced merchant-and-pricing layer, a more deliberate loot-distribution framework, or a harsher scarcity-driven progression model.

### Baseline

- **Trade and Barter** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/23081)) — Main baseline. Merchant-pricing route. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **C.O.I.N. - Coins of Interesting Nature** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/51439)) — Lightweight economic texture layer adding regional coin variety without touching prices or loot distribution. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **C.O.I.N. - Treasury Exchange** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/131682)) — Currency exchange companion adding mechanical purpose to C.O.I.N. by letting merchants convert coin types. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Roleplaying in Skyrim - Encounter Zones** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/149899)) — Optional encounter zone overhaul reworking all encounter zones for realism and variety with a randomizer. No hard requirements; ESL flagged. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Roleplaying in Skyrim - Evolving Economy** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/149830)) — Optional dynamic pricing layer where barter costs fluctuate based on resources, season, Civil War, reputation, and distance. Requires SKSE, `Papyrus Ini Manipulator`, and `powerofthree's Papyrus Extender`. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Roleplaying in Skyrim - Loot Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/157259)) — Optional rarity-driven, contextually-aware loot overhaul with equipment breakage and deleveled artifacts. Requires SKSE, `Papyrus Ini Manipulator`, and `SkyUI`. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Narrative Loot** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/12812)) — Optional environmental-storytelling layer adding 1,629 lore-friendly miscellaneous items to loot lists for world-building texture. Does not change economy balance, prices, or weapon/armor distribution. Stacks cleanly with `Trade and Barter`, `C.O.I.N.`, or any other economy baseline. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **SUDs - Skyrim's Unique Drinks** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/85824)) — Optional beverage-content expansion. Adds 50+ unique drink items (meads, wines, spirits, teas) with custom meshes, textures, and effects. Distributes through leveled lists and world placement. Complements Gourmet's food scope with a dedicated drink layer. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Alternatives

- **Open World Loot** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/49681)) — Deliberate structure-focused comparison if the project wants loot progression and encounter rewards to feel more curated. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **MorrowLoot Ultimate** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/3058)) — Heavy scarcity comparison only if the list truly wants rare-item access and deleveled loot as a defining identity pillar. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Notes

- A heavy loot-distribution framework can quietly absorb crafting and progression ownership before later sections decide.
- A pricing-only route can undershoot if loot progression remains too close to vanilla.
- Harsh scarcity can make exploration, crafting, and reward pacing feel punitive rather than immersive.
- Treat pricing changes and loot-distribution changes as related but distinct philosophies.
- `Barter Limit Fix` belongs in `modlist-foundations.md` (Targeted Bugfix Mods), not as the economy-framework answer. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

## Artifact And Unique Item Overhauls → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection owns the unique-item and artifact content packs that add depth to the named items the player finds in the world: Daedric artifacts, dragon priest masks, unique weapons, and miscellaneous quest items. `Elder Wilds` treats these mods as a separate ownership layer from crafting because artifact overhauls change what named items are, not how the player makes generic items. The right baseline is balanced-and-lore-friendly — these mods should make discoveries feel meaningful without breaking the loot ladder.

### Baseline

- **Reliquary of Myth - Artifacts of Skyrim** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/31612)) — Artifact-stat and effect baseline. Rebalances nearly all of Skyrim's legendary artifacts with unique enchantments, new stats, and lore-friendly effects. Canonical mid-size artifact overhaul (12K endorsements, last updated March 2025). → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **ArteFakes - Unique Artifacts Replacer** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/41254)) — Model-replacement companion. Swaps the actual 3D models of unique items for higher-detail versions. Stacks cleanly with `Reliquary of Myth` (stats vs meshes). → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Konahrik's Accoutrements** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/22206)) — Dragon-priest-specific companion. Upgrades named Dragon Priest encounters with new gear, masks with their own stat curves, and harder fights. The only one that adds new encounters and the only one the Legacy of the Dragonborn museum integration cares about directly. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Unique Items Tweaks - Improved Miscellaneous Artifacts** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/33723)) — Gap-filler improving miscellaneous artifacts `Reliquary of Myth` does not cover. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Artificer - An Artifact Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/99619)) — Simonrim-aligned alternative to `Reliquary of Myth`. Requires `Thaumaturgy` and `Mysticism` as prerequisites (both locked). Prefer if full Simonrim ecosystem coherence and the most recently maintained option (last updated April 2026) matter more. Choose one artifact overhaul baseline — not both. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Alternatives

- **Discipline-first route** — Rely on vanilla artifact stats. Vanilla artifacts are functional but very front-loaded toward late-game Daedric items.

### Notes

- Stacking `Reliquary of Myth` with `ArteFakes` and `Unique Items Tweaks` touches every unique-item record in the game. They are designed to stack, but verify the Synthesis patcher chain in `Performance` does not re-balance the same records. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Konahrik's Accoutrements` adds new Dragon Priest encounters that may not be patched into Legacy of the Dragonborn's display system. Check the `LoTD Patches (Official)` hub in `modlist-lotd.md` for an integration patch. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- Artifact overhauls that rebalance too aggressively can break encounter expectations. Verify Wuuthrad, Mehrunes' Razor, and the Gauldur weapons feel right before lock-in.
- `Unique Items Tweaks` overlaps lightly with `Reliquary of Myth` on a small number of records. Verify with a Bashed Patch run. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

## Weapon Pack Additions → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection owns the weapon content packs that add new named and leveled-list weapons to the world: swords, axes, maces, bows, and specialty weapons that fit vanilla's medieval-Nordic aesthetic. It is kept separate from `Smithing And Crafting Expansion` (crafting system) and the weapon-quality-overhaul layer in `modlist-survival-combat.md` (balance). This subsection owns content variety. The right baseline is "more variety without diluting the loot ladder".

### Baseline

- **Heavy Armory - New Weapons** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/6308)) — Content-variety baseline. Adds 150+ lore-friendly weapons across 11 new types (halberds, spears, glaives, shortswords, quarterstaves, mauls, hatchets, clubs, long maces, shortspears, tridents) with OAR-compatible custom animations for polearms and leveled-list integration. Actively maintained (v6.1.6, 16.8K endorsements). → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **LeanWolf's Better-Shaped Weapons SE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/2017)) — Mesh-quality companion upgrading the silhouette of vanilla weapons. Stacks cleanly with `Heavy Armory` (different records). → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Legendary Skyrim Crossbows and Bows SSE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/8273)) — Specialty route for crossbow-and-bow archetype. Adds new crossbow and bow models with named and unique variants. Lock only if ranged combat is a viable build path. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Alternatives

- **Immersive Weapons** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/16788)) — Legacy comparison for maximum raw weapon count (230+). Unmaintained since April 2018. Synthesis patcher chain in `Performance` has explicit `Bash Tags` for it. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Discipline-first route** — Rely on vanilla weapons only.

### Notes

- `Heavy Armory` uses standard leveled-list integration and does not require synthesis patcher intervention the way `Immersive Weapons` does. A dedicated `Heavy Armory - Adamant Addon` (mod 144316) provides Simonrim-aligned perk integration. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `LeanWolf's Better-Shaped Weapons` can conflict with `Unique Items Tweaks` and `Reliquary of Myth` on unique-weapon records. Verify model swaps do not affect the visual identity of iconic unique items. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Legendary Skyrim Crossbows and Bows` makes crossbows a meaningful archetype — `modlist-survival-combat.md` difficulty decisions need to account for ranged damage output. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- All three weapon mods add new models — `Pandora` and `BodySlide` rebuild triggers in `Performance` fire whenever any of them is updated. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

## Armor Pack Additions → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection owns the armor content packs that add new named and leveled-list armors to the world: light, heavy, and specialty armors that fit the medieval-Nordic aesthetic of vanilla Skyrim. The right baseline is the same as the weapons subsection: more variety without diluting the loot ladder.

### Baseline

- **Immersive Armors** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/3479)) — Content-variety baseline. Adds 200+ new armors (light, heavy, mage, fur, leather) with leveled-list integration. Canonical armor content pack (206K endorsements). Synthesis patcher chain in `Performance` has explicit `Bash Tags` for it. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **NordwarUA Total Armor and Weapon Compilation** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/61423)) — Vanilla-armor model overhaul compiling six NordwarUA mods. Standardizes cubemaps and removes stat changes, providing a visual upgrade without NPC balance changes. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Common Clothes and Armors** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/21305)) — Light-armor-diversity companion adding clothing-and-light-armor variants for bandits, sellswords, and travelers. The clothing-side companion to `NordwarUA`. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Bandolier - Bags and Pouches Classic** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/2417)) — Carry-capacity utility layer. Adds bags, pouches, and bandoliers with MCM configuration. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Immersive Armors Retexture and Mesh Fixes SE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/75891)) — Required visual companion. Fixes broken meshes, outdated textures, and UV maps for IA sets. Foundation that other IA mesh mods build on. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Immersive Armours - SSE CBBE 3BA BodySlide** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/22382)) — BodySlide files for all female IA outfits. Standalone conversion that replaces IA's original female meshes. Choose one female conversion path — do not stack with the Retexture and Mesh Fixes mod's bundled CBBE meshes. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Immersive Armor HIMBO Conversion V2** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/76552)) — BodySlide conversion of all IA armors for HIMBO. Designed to stack on top of the Retexture and Mesh Fixes mod. Includes CBBE compatibility patch. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Immersive Armors - Realistic Armors replace SE by Xtudo** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/36746)) — Optional art-direction patch swapping 10 IA sets for NordWarUA's Realistic Armors. Addresses "too much The Witcher" criticism. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

**Stack order for a full IA visual pipeline:** `Immersive Armors` → `Immersive Armors Retexture and Mesh Fixes SE` → (optional) `IA - Realistic Armors replace by Xtudo` → (optional, choose one female path) `IA CBBE 3BA BodySlide` OR the CBBE compatibility patch bundled with `IA HIMBO Conversion V2` → (optional, if male body) `IA HIMBO Conversion V2`. Run BodySlide after all IA mods are installed, then rebuild all IA batches.

### Alternatives

- **Discipline-first route** — Rely on vanilla armor sets only.

### Notes

- `Immersive Armors` is approaching a decade unmaintained (last updated November 2016) — verify game version compatibility before locking. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `NordwarUA Total Armor` standardizes cubemaps across all six sub-mods — any mod that depended on the original cubemaps needs re-testing. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Common Clothes and Armors` adds clothing to NPC leveled lists. If `Armor and Clothing Extension` is also in the list, verify NPCs look right after both are active. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Bandolier` changes the inventory system — carry-weight design needs to account for additional slots. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- The Retexture and Mesh Fixes mod and the CBBE 3BA BodySlide conversion are **not** stackable for female meshes. The Retexture and Mesh Fixes author explicitly warns against mixing. The HIMBO Conversion V2 avoids this conflict because it is designed to stack on top of the Retexture and Mesh Fixes mod.
- The Realistic Armors replace mod requires `Realistic Armor` (36151) as a hard dependency alongside `Immersive Armors`. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- All IA companion mods add new models — `Pandora` and `BodySlide` rebuild triggers in `Performance` fire whenever any of them is updated. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

This subsection owns the clothing content packs that add new clothing, cloaks, and wearable non-armor items to the world: common clothes, noble clothes, cloaks, and backpacks. The right baseline is "more wardrobe variety without making every NPC a fashion show".

### Baseline

- **Armor and Clothing Extension** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/19002)) — Clothing-diversity baseline. Adds lore-friendly clothing options and NPC-appropriate attire (35K+ endorsements). Requires `RUSTIC CLOTHING - SE` (section 02) and `SkyUI` (section 01) — both already locked. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Common Clothes and Armors** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/21305)) — Common-cloth variety companion pairing with ACE to give bandit-and-sellsword archetypes real visual variety. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Sforzinda Imitations** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/54346)) — Handmade-outfit branch for curated boutique clothing. Not automatic baseline coverage but the cleanest verified addition for distinct crafted outfits. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Cloaks of Skyrim** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/6369)) — Cloak-slot addition. Adds 100+ new cloaks for player and NPCs via SPID distribution. The most visually impactful wearable slot in third-person. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- **Colovian Noble Clothes** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/4464)) — High-status clothing pass adding noble and Imperial-themed clothing. Pairs with Beyond Skyrim content. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`

### Alternatives

- **Discipline-first route** — Rely on vanilla clothing only.

### Notes

- `Armor and Clothing Extension` requires `RUSTIC CLOTHING - SE` and `SkyUI` — both already in the list. Verify load order keeps `RUSTIC CLOTHING` above ACE. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Common Clothes and Armors` is referenced in both this subsection and the armor subsection above. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Sforzinda Imitations` is a curated boutique pack — treat as an explicit add-on branch, not automatic baseline coverage. If it pulls the list toward a boutique-fantasy wardrobe tone, cut it. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Cloaks of Skyrim` adds 100+ new cloak items. If `modlist-third-person.md` adds a custom cloak-slot overhaul, verify the two stack cleanly. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Colovian Noble Clothes` is a smaller mod with less community-maintenance footprint — verify the mod page for known issues before locking. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
- `Knapsack Backpacks SE` (cosmetic) and `Bandolier` (inventory) can stack, but verify the inventory UI does not show duplicate bag icons. → `Expanded Systems - Crafting & Economy`
