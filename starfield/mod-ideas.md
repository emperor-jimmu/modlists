## Considered (Creations-only or out of scope)

Mods that surfaced during research but are not in the Nexus/MO2 load order.

### Real Lights Starfield (RabbitDoesStuff)

- **Category:** Graphics (lighting)
- **Concept:** 15,000+ hand-placed light sources, environmental enhancements, lore-friendly quests.
- **Rationale:** The most comprehensive Starfield lighting overhaul.
- **Status:** Creations-only — noted as optional in the Wave 1 graphics section.

### The Hatch - A Horror Quest (GlueMods)

- **Category:** Content (quest)
- **Concept:** Free horror questline, begun at the Jemison Mercantile Storeroom.
- **Rationale:** Highly regarded horror quest.
- **Status:** Creations-only — not in the MO2 list.

### Lyria / Robin Locke (voiced companions)

- **Category:** Content (characters)
- **Concept:** Fully voiced companions with affinity and personal quests.
- **Rationale:** The most popular voiced companions.
- **Status:** Paid Creations — out of scope for this Nexus/MO2 list.

### Star Wars Genesis (total conversion)

- **Category:** Content (total conversion)
- **Concept:** Empire vs. Rebellion storylines, new planets, Jedi powers, expanded RPG systems.
- **Rationale:** The flagship total conversion, usually installed via Wabbajack.
- **Status:** Idea — too large a departure for the current two-wave structure; candidate for a future dedicated wave.

### Starfield Community Patch (SFCP)

- **Category:** Mechanics (bug fixes)
- **Concept:** Open-source, community-owned bug-fix collection.
- **Rationale:** Standard "unofficial patch" role.
- **Status:** Deferred — maintenance uncertainty (mid-2025 onward) and Bethesda's official fixes make it optional for 1.16.244; revisit if the project stabilizes.
