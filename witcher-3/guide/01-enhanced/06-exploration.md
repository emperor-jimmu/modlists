## The ? System

Every undiscovered location on the map shows as a white question mark (?). These include:
- **Places of Power** -- grant a free skill point on first use
- **Hidden treasures** -- guarded loot (often relic gear)
- **Monster nests** -- destroy with Igni for XP and materials
- **Abandoned sites** -- clear monsters to restore the village
- **Smuggler's caches** -- floating barrels in water (especially in Skellige)

## Region Breakdown

### White Orchard (Tutorial Zone)
Fully explore before leaving -- this area becomes partially inaccessible after the main quest progresses past a certain point. There are 6 Places of Power here, giving you 6 free skill points to start the game strong. The Temerian armor set (purchased from the merchant near the inn) is the best early-game gear.

### Velen / Novigrad
The largest region with 200+ ? locations. Many smugglers caches are in the water -- use the boat or swim with the crossbow equipped. Bandit camps, monster nests, and abandoned sites populate the countryside. Hidden quests trigger when you discover certain locations.

### Skellige
An archipelago of 6+ islands. Approximately 180 ? locations, the vast majority of which are underwater smuggler's caches. Sailing is required. Bring patience for the boat travel -- there is a fast travel point between the main islands. High-level monsters (30+) inhabit remote islands.

### Toussaint (Blood and Wine)
The endgame zone with the highest-level content. Hanse bases are repeatable combat encounters great for farming XP and crowns. New Gwent cards, grandmaster crafting, and the most beautiful scenery in the game.

## Treasure Hunt Tips

- Check every notice board for treasure map pins
- Witcher gear scavenger hunts appear automatically at appropriate levels
- Hidden treasure POIs often have relic gear
- Some treasures are level-scaled to when you find them -- save them for later if you want higher-level gear
