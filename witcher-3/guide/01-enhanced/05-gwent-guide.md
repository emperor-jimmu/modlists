## Gwent Basics

Gwent is a card game played over three rounds. Each player draws from a deck of unit cards, special cards, and hero cards.

### Rules
- Each round, players take turns playing one card
- Pass to end your turn (skip remaining rounds)
- Win 2 out of 3 rounds to win the match
- Each faction has unique strengths

## Factions

|---------|-----------|-----------|

| Nilfgaardian Empire | Spy manipulation, high stats | Stefan Skellen, Cahir |
| Scoia'tael | Weak-but-numerous units, ambush | Milva, Toruviel |
| Monsters | Swarm tactics, unit cloning | Vampire: Katakan, Crone |
| Skellige | Graveyard synergy, two-round burst | Cerys, Madman Lugos |

## Deck Building Tips

1. **Minimum 22 unit cards** - fill with your best
2. **No more than 10 special cards** - decoys, horns, scorch
3. **Include spies** for card advantage
4. **Medics** resurrect key cards
5. **Weather cards** to counter opponent's row
