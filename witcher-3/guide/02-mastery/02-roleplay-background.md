# Roleplay Background

<span class="wave-badge wave-2">Wave 2</span>

## Master Witcher: The Legend of the White Wolf

The stories say a witcher lived who never lost a contract. Who faced down a leshen with nothing but a silver sword and a worn-out sign. Who walked into vilager camps covered in monster blood and walked out with gold in his pocket and a new tale on his lips.

That witcher is you.

### Background

You've been walking the Path for decades. The Trial of the Grasses is a distant memory, Kaer Morhen a fading dream. You've seen the best and worst of the Continent - the nobility and the filth, the beauty and the horror, the order and the chaos.

Now the monsters have adapted. They're smarter, faster, deadlier. Old tactics fail. New ones must be forged.

### Character Concept

- **School**: Your choice (Wolf, Cat, Bear, Griffin, Viper, Manticore)
- **Age**: Older, wiser, slower but more precise
- **Motto**: "Monsters come in many shapes. The worst ones wear human faces."
- **Playstyle**: Methodical, prepared, never reckless

### Suggested Playthrough Rules

1. Always prepare before contracts - read bestiary, apply oils
2. Never use fast travel except between regions
3. Meditation only at inns or campsites
4. Wear your school's witcher gear
5. Complete contracts before main quests
6. No Quen spam - treat each sign as situational
