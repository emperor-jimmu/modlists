# Combat Basics

<span class="wave-badge wave-0">Wave 0</span>

## Core Combat Flow

Witcher combat is about **preparation and positioning**, not button-mashing. Each fight follows a pattern:

1. Read the bestiary before the contract -- know your enemy's weaknesses
2. Apply the right oil to your silver sword -- +50% damage against that type
3. Cast Quen before engaging -- absorbs one hit
4. Use the right Sign during the fight
5. Dodge and reposition -- never stand still

## Attack Types

**Light Attack** -- Fast, chains into a 3-hit combo. Low stamina cost. Your primary damage source against most enemies.

**Heavy Attack** -- Slow but powerful. Breaks guards and staggers. High stamina cost. Best against single, slow enemies.

**Parry** -- Blocks incoming attacks. Only works against humanoid enemies. Timing is forgiving.

**Counter** -- Tap Parry just as an enemy's attack lands. Staggers them briefly. Does not work on most monsters.

**Dodge** -- Quick sidestep. Low stamina cost. Your main defensive move.

**Roll** -- Long-distance evasion with invincibility frames. Higher stamina cost. Use for large-area attacks.

## The Five Signs

| Sign | Effect | Best Against |
|------|--------|--------------|
| **Aard** | Telekinetic blast, knocks back/stuns | Shielded humans, group packs |
| **Igni** | Fire damage, burning status | Necrophages, monster nests |
| **Yrden** | Magic trap, slows enemies | Wraiths, foglets, fast enemies |
| **Quen** | Protective damage shield | Everything (always useful) |
| **Axii** | Stuns / mind-control | Humans, conversation checks |

::: tip
**Quen is the most important Sign for new players.** Keep it active at all times. It absorbs one hit completely and buys you time to react.
:::

## Combat Survival Tips

1. **Never fight fair** -- use every tool: bombs, traps, potions, the environment
2. **Dodge toward attacks**, not away -- many enemies have forward-reaching swings that miss if you step inside their range
3. **Igni destroys monster nests** -- you get XP and materials
4. **Aard knocks down** -- follow up with a one-hit kill prompt on grounded enemies
5. **The crossbow** -- one-shots flying enemies out of the sky, useful underwater
