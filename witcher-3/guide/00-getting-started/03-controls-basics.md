## User Interface Walkthrough

- **Inventory Screen (I)** -- equipment, consumables, crafting materials, books, quest items
- **Character Screen (C)** -- skill tree, attributes, character stats, mutagen slots
- **Map Screen (M)** -- world map, fast travel, quest markers, legend
- **Journal (J)** -- quest log, bestiary, glossary, characters, books
- **Alchemy Screen (B)** -- potions, bombs, oils formulas (learn new ones from diagrams)
- **Bestiary** -- auto-populates as you encounter enemies. Read it for weaknesses

## Movement & Navigation

### Roach (Your Horse)
- Summon by holding the Whistle key
- Gallop by holding sprint -- Roach follows roads automatically
- Combat on horseback: attack with sword, use crossbow for mounted combat
- Roach can be called from surprisingly far away, but won't cross water

### Fast Travel
- Unlock signposts by interacting with them once
- Open the world map and click any unlocked signpost
- Fast travel is disabled during combat
- Some interior areas require leaving before fast travelling
