# Progression Guide

<span class="wave-badge wave-0">Wave 0</span>

## How Leveling Works

You gain experience (XP) from:
- **Main quests** -- largest XP rewards (100-500 XP)
- **Side quests** -- significant XP (50-200 XP)
- **Witcher contracts** -- good XP and coin (50-150 XP)
- **Discovering locations** -- 5-10 XP per ? cleared
- **Killing enemies** -- minimal XP (1-20 XP)

The game uses soft level scaling. Quests have recommended levels, and enemies within a few levels of you are fair fights.

## Skill Trees

There are four skill trees, plus mutations unlocked later:

| Tree | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Combat** | Sword damage, critical hits, adrenaline gain |
| **Signs** | Magic intensity, alternate Sign modes |
| **Alchemy** | Potion duration, toxicity, bomb effectiveness |
| **General** | Utility perks, cross-tree bonuses |

## Beginner Build (Levels 1-15)

Place 3 ability points into **Muscle Memory** (Combat, fast attack damage). Then 3 into **Precise Blows** (Combat, fast attack crit chance). This gives you reliable damage.

For survivability, place 1 point into **Gourmet** (General) -- it makes food regenerate health for 20 minutes instead of 10 seconds, effectively giving you passive healing through most of the early game.

Finish with 3 points into **Exploding Shield** (Signs) to make your Quen shield damage nearby enemies when broken.

## Gear Progression

1. **White Orchard** (levels 1-6) -- loot everything, buy the Temerian armor set from the merchant near the inn
2. **Velen / Novigrad** (levels 6-18) -- start Witcher gear scavenger hunts (Griffin School gear)
3. **Skellige** (levels 18-30) -- upgrade to Cat or Bear School gear
4. **Kaer Morhen and beyond** (levels 30+) -- enhanced, superior, and mastercrafted witcher gear

Always replace gear that is 5+ levels below you. Relic items (orange name) have unique bonuses but are often outclassed by witcher gear of the same level.

## Mutagens

Mutagens provide passive bonuses when slotted:

- **Red mutagen** -- +Attack Power
- **Green mutagen** -- +Alchemy bonuses
- **Blue mutagen** -- +Sign Intensity

Slot three skills of the same color adjacent to a matching mutagen for a bonus multiplier (e.g., 3 red skills + red mutagen = 3x the mutagen's effect). Upgrade mutagens to Greater versions (3 of the same type) for even stronger effects.
