# Map & Environment

Visual and terrain improvements for the game map.

## Clear Map

- **Link:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2847198345
- **Mod Version:** 1.19.x
- **CK3 Version:** 1.19.x
- **Updated:** 2026-06-12
- **Load Order:** Early
- **Essential:** Yes
- **Performance:** None

Removes the fog of war overlay on the map, making all terrain, holdings, and borders clearly visible even in areas you don't control.

**Gameplay Impact:** Purely visual — makes map exploration and planning easier. Does not reveal hidden information (FOW remains on characters and armies).

## Brighter Water

- **Link:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2856891234
- **Mod Version:** 1.2.0
- **CK3 Version:** 1.14.x+
- **Updated:** 2026-02-15
- **Load Order:** Early
- **Essential:** No
- **Performance:** None

Replaces the vanilla dark/oily water texture with brighter, clearer water. Matches the aesthetic of terrain overhaul mods.

**Gameplay Impact:** Pure visual. Makes coastal regions and rivers more pleasant to look at.

## Immersive Toponyms

- **Link:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2255229872
- **Mod Version:** 1.19.x
- **CK3 Version:** 1.19.x
- **Updated:** 2026-05-15
- **Load Order:** Mid
- **Essential:** No
- **Performance:** Minor

Adds ahistorical place name localizations alongside historical ones. Conquered provinces can display names in your culture's language. Standalone mod — not compatible with other toponymy mods like Rosetta or MCN.

**Gameplay Impact:** More immersive map with culturally appropriate place names. Incompatible with other toponymy mods.

## Cities of Wonder 3

- **Link:** https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2901477267
- **Mod Version:** 1.18.1
- **CK3 Version:** 1.18.x (retired — not updated to 1.19)
- **Updated:** 2026-04-01
- **Load Order:** Late
- **Essential:** No
- **Performance:** Moderate

County capitals can become Cities of Wonder through organic growth or a costly decision. Each city gains unique modifiers and 3D models as development rises. Achievement and Ironman compatible.

**Gameplay Impact:** Adds a city growth system that rewards developing your realm. Note: the author has retired CK3 development — check for 1.19 update or use the newer CK3 sprawl system instead.

## Medieval Arts

- **Link:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2452585382
- **Mod Version:** 1.19.x
- **CK3 Version:** 1.19.x
- **Updated:** 2026-06-15
- **Load Order:** Mid
- **Essential:** No
- **Performance:** Moderate

Adds 100+ historically-inspired 3D monuments, buildings, and map objects across the world. Cathedrals, castles, mosques, pagodas, and wonders appear on the map as unique 3D models.

**Gameplay Impact:** Purely visual. The map becomes dramatically more detailed and immersive. Each major city and historical site has a unique appearance.

## A Special World — Special Building Compatch

- **Link:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2875587269
- **Mod Version:** 1.19.x
- **CK3 Version:** 1.19.x
- **Updated:** 2026-06-10
- **Load Order:** Late (after Medieval Arts, MB+, RICE)
- **Essential:** No
- **Performance:** Minor

Compatch that ensures special buildings from Medieval Arts, Cities of Wonder 3, Unique Buildings+, RICE, More Bookmarks+, Africa Plus, and Muslim Enhancements display correctly together without overlapping or missing models.

**Gameplay Impact:** Fixes visual conflicts between map-enhancing mods. Required if running multiple building/monument mods together.
