## Choosing a Landing Tile
<!-- TODO: Explain key tile features:
- Geothermal vents: free, infinite power later. Huge advantage.
- Fertile soil: crops grow faster. Matters a lot.
- Road access: faster caravan travel, easier trading.
- Mountains: can dig into mountain for safe base (infestation risk late game).
- Rivers: natural defensive barrier against raiders.
- Proximity to neutral/friendly settlements: trading partners.
Recommendation: find a tile with at least 2 geothermal vents, some fertile soil, near a road. -->

## Early Game (Days 1-15)
<!-- TODO: Explain first priorities:
- Hour 1: Create a room (even a wooden shack) with beds. Assign sleeping spots.
- Day 1-2: Start growing rice (fastest crop). Start cutting wood.
- Day 2-5: Build a proper base. Walls, separate rooms, a freezer.
- Day 3-7: Research. Prioritize: batteries → solar panels → stone cutting.
- Raid 1 (day ~5-10): They come. Have your pawns equipped. Use terrain and doors for cover.
- Day 7-15: Stone cutting (replace wooden walls). More growing. Second colonist from join event.
Key mistakes to avoid: building too big too fast, not having a freezer, wooden base (one fire = disaster). -->

## Mid Game (Days 15-100)
<!-- TODO: Explain:
- Stable power grid (solar + batteries + geothermal)

- Caravans for trading
- Component and steel crafting
- Multiple research benches

## DLC Breakdown
<!-- TODO: Explain each DLC:
- **Royalty**: Empire faction, psycasts (magic-like powers), nobility titles. Adds quests. Good to add after first playthrough.
- **Ideology**: Belief systems, rituals, roles, memes. Huge depth. Adds complexity to every decision. Add after you're comfortable.
- **Biotech**: Children, mechanitors (controlling mechs), xenogenes (genetic races). Best integrated DLC — fine to enable on first play.
- **Anomaly**: Horror theme, void monsters, monolith events. Adds dangerous new threats. Wait until you've defended against regular raids.
Recommendation for first run: enable Biotech only. Or enable all but expect complexity. -->
