### DLC Liberation Protocol

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/12322>
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Released:** 2024-01-14
- **Summary:** Integrates Twitch & GOG exclusive items (Gwynbleidd, Scorch, Yasha, Black Unicorn, NUSA Infiltrator set, Witcher/GOG-themed clothing) into the world as discoverable loot and vendor items.
- **Why:** Exclusive promo items are normally locked behind Twitch drops or GOG connections that most players never see. This integrates them into the game world through treasure-hunt scenarios and clothing vendors, turning corporate exclusives into rewarding exploration finds.
- **Conflicts:** None known
- **Dependencies:** ArchiveXL, RED4ext, TweakXL
- **MO2 Notes:** Extract to game root. Four discovery locations (Black Unicorn in base game, others in Phantom Liberty zones). NUSA Infiltrator set sold at Stylishly in Heywood; Witcher/GOG items at Pacifica clothing shop.
- **Performance:** None

### Extra Iconics

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/15889>
- **Version:** 2.1.0
- **Released:** 2025-10-12
- **Summary:** 17 new iconic weapons with custom appearances, VFX, and original gameplay effects scattered across Night City at vendors, hidden locations, and quest areas.
- **Why:** Vanilla has a limited set of iconic weapons — this doubles the pool with handcrafted, lore-friendly weapons that have unique mechanics (plasma shotgun, sentient ghost blade, EMP thunderstrike LMG, etc.). Each weapon has a distinct playstyle and discovery method.
- **Conflicts:** Incompatible with standalone versions of included weapons (uninstall old versions first)
- **Dependencies:** ArchiveXL, Codeware, redscript, Trigger Mode Control, TweakXL
- **MO2 Notes:** Extract to game root. Files go to `r6/scripts/SeijaxIconics`, `r6/tweaks/SeijaxIconics`, and `archive/pc/mod/`. To uninstall, remove all three paths. Translations available as separate downloads. Item codes available in description for troubleshooting.
- **Performance:** Light (script-only, no runtime overhead)

### Nomad Cache - New Iconic Weapons

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/19234>
- **Version:** 1.0.1
- **Released:** 2025-01-31
- **Summary:** Three Nomad-themed iconic weapons with custom appearances and effects: Inferno-V8R (Warden LMG), Trailblazer (Satara tech shotgun), Tepéw (poison tomahawk). Purchased from Badlands vendors.
- **Why:** The Badlands and Nomad lifestyle are underrepresented in iconic weapons. These weapons have thematically appropriate mechanics (overheat, solar charging, rattler venom) that reward Nomad-style exploration of the Badlands.
- **Conflicts:** None known
- **Dependencies:** ArchiveXL, Codeware, RED4ext, redscript, Trigger Mode Control (required for Trailblazer), TweakXL. Requires Phantom Liberty.
- **MO2 Notes:** Extract to game root. Weapons purchased from specific Badlands vendors. Optional files: Enhanced Cooling (disables self-burn), Hellhound (disables sprint cleanse). Alternate acquisition via Mr. Blue Eyes vendor mod (#22696) or CET commands.
- **Performance:** None

### Mox Cache - New Iconic Weapons

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/20073>
- **Version:** 1.0.2
- **Released:** 2025-08-20
- **Summary:** Three Mox-themed iconic weapons: Sloppy Seconds (Guillotine SMG with chain bounces), Glitch Bitch (Achilles tech rifle with micro-taser debuff), and a secret third weapon. Found in Kabuki and Lizzie's Bar.
- **Why:** The Mox are a core Night City faction with zero iconic representation. These weapons have mechanically unique effects (ricochet chaining, embedded tasers) that fit the Mox's DIY, punk aesthetic.
- **Conflicts:** None known
- **Dependencies:** ArchiveXL, Codeware, RED4ext, redscript, TweakXL. Requires Phantom Liberty.
- **MO2 Notes:** Extract to game root. Sloppy Seconds looted from NCPD Scanner Hustle south of Kabuki Market. Glitch Bitch purchased from Lizzie's Bar bartender. Secret third weapon on Jig-Jig Street. Alternate acquisition via CET commands or Mr. Blue Eyes vendor.
- **Performance:** None
