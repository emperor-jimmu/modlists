### Zenitex Modular Military Backpack - ArchiveXL

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/18152>
- **Version:** 1.5
- **Released:** 2024-12-07
- **Summary:** Modular backpack system with 60 items for both Vs. Dynamic mesh, garment support, gameplay bonuses, and lore-friendly design. ArchiveXL-powered equipment slot.
- **Why:** Adds a visible, functional equipment layer to V's silhouette — backpacks that provide gameplay bonuses rather than pure cosmetics. The Zenitex ecosystem is well-maintained and lore-friendly, fitting Night City's militarized aesthetic.
- **Conflicts:** None known. 0 bugs reported.
- **Dependencies:** ArchiveXL, Codeware, Equipment-EX, RED4ext, redscript, TweakXL, Zenitex Core (#16356). Recommended: Zenitex Virtual Atelier (#10090), The Zenitex Military Store (#21735) for in-game purchasing.
- **MO2 Notes:** Extract to game root. Tag as **Root**. Install after Equipment-EX and Zenitex Core. Purchase via Zenitex Virtual Atelier or Military Store. 3 files available (main mod + optional add-ons). Both male and female V supported.
- **Performance:** None
