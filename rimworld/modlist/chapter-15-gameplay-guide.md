# Chapter 15: Gameplay Guide — A Full Modded Run

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the Wave 1 → Wave 2 progression in practice
- Apply all previous mod chapters in a cohesive playthrough
- Navigate early, mid, and late game with an expanding modlist

## Overview

This chapter walks through a complete playthrough using the full modlist, organized by the two-wave system introduced in Chapter 1.

- **Wave 1 (Ch 3-7)** — Install before starting. These mods fix bugs, polish UI, improve performance, and upgrade graphics/audio. They don't change how RimWorld plays — they make it run better and look better.
- **Wave 2 (Ch 8-14)** — Add gradually as your colony matures. Each wave 2 mod adds new systems, content, or depth. The sections below tell you when to add each one.

Start a new colony with only Wave 1 mods enabled. Play through the early and mid game. As you reach each milestone below, activate the corresponding Wave 2 mods in RimPy, run auto-sort, and save.

## Starting Your Modded Colony (Wave 1 Only)

Install all Wave 1 mods (Ch 3-7) before creating your colony. With RimPy, activate them, sort, save, and launch.

**Colonist selection** — Wave 1 mods add no new traits, so the vanilla selection advice from Chapter 1 still applies. RimHUD (Ch 5) shows you all pawn stats on one screen — use it to evaluate colonists faster than the vanilla info card.

**Biome** — Stick with Temperate Forest for your first modded run. More Vanilla Biomes (Ch 11, Wave 2) is available later but your first colony should be on familiar terrain.

**Initial base layout** — With Wave 1 only, your building options are vanilla. That's deliberate. Learn the vanilla build patterns (freezer, killbox, hospital, prison) before adding Vanilla Furniture Expanded (Ch 9, Wave 2). The Designator Shapes tool (Ch 5) helps plan rooms, and Better Workbench Management (Ch 5) keeps your production lines organised from day one.

**What you'll notice on day one:**
- The game runs smoother (RocketMan, Performance Optimizer from Ch 4).
- UI elements are larger and more readable at 4K (Camera+, RimHUD from Ch 5).
- Sound is cleaner (No Time Change Sounds from Ch 7).
- Pawns automatically avoid building themselves into corners (Smarter Construction from Ch 8).
- You can click Allow All to unforbid everything after the initial crash landing (Allow Tool from Ch 5).

## Early Game (Days 1-30) — Wave 1 Active

Your first 30 days are about survival — food, shelter, basic defenses. Wave 1 mods make this phase smoother without changing what you need to learn.

**What Wave 1 mods are doing:**
- **RocketMan** (Ch 4) is optimising tick speed in the background. You won't notice it until you have 30+ animals, at which point you'll be grateful.
- **Allow Tool** (Ch 5): After every fight, click and drag a selection over all dropped items, then click "Allow All." This unforbids every dropped weapon, chunk, and piece of apparel with one click instead of clicking each item individually.
- **RimHUD** (Ch 5): Click any pawn and see their shooting accuracy, melee DPS, move speed, armour, mood, and all skills without switching tabs. You'll use this constantly.
- **Camera+** (Ch 5): Zoom out to survey your entire base, zoom in to inspect pawn expressions. At 4K, the extended zoom-out is essential for spotting raids at the map edge.
- **Range Finder** (Ch 5): Before your first raid, select your shooters and press the hotkey to see their weapon range as a coloured overlay. Position them so their range covers the approach path.
- **Smarter Construction** (Ch 8): Your builder won't trap themselves inside walls. One less frustration to worry about while you learn the basics.

**What NOT to install yet:**
- Don't add Vanilla Furniture Expanded (Ch 9) yet — learn vanilla room layouts first.
- Don't add creature mods (Ch 12) — vanilla animals are enough for the early game.
- Don't add Hospitality (Ch 13) — you don't have spare beds or surplus food.
- Don't add Dubs Bad Hygiene (Ch 8) — plumbing is a mid-game infrastructure project.

**Tip:** This is the best time to experiment. Your colony is small, stakes are low, and mistakes are recoverable. Try different base layouts, learn how the work tab prioritises jobs, and get comfortable drafting pawns for combat.

## Mid Game (Days 30-120) — Adding Wave 2 Mods

Your colony is stable: food is sustainable, you have 6-10 colonists, basic defenses are up, and you've survived at least one serious raid. Now it's time to add depth.

**Phase 1: Add furniture and production (Ch 9)**

Activate the Vanilla Furniture Expanded modules in RimPy, sort, and save. The new workbenches, linkables, and furniture appear in your build menu immediately.

- Build dedicated workshops with linkable buildings — the adjacency bonuses make production noticeably faster.
- Replace wooden walls with stone (VFE Architect adds reinforced walls for extra durability).
- Upgrade your power grid with VFE Power's industrial solar panels and chemfuel generators.
- Add the Medical Module to upgrade your hospital.

**Phase 2: Add gameplay depth (Ch 8 — Deep Systems)**

Install **Dubs Bad Hygiene** once you have a stable power grid and spare construction capacity. Plumbing needs planning: lay water pipes before flooring, position bathrooms near bedrooms, and build a sewage outlet away from your water source. The initial setup takes an hour of in-game time but becomes second nature after one colony.

Install **Prison Labor** when you have a dedicated prison wing. Turn prisoners into miners, crafters, and cleaners. Build a proper prison with recreation and good food to keep them productive and reduce escape attempts.

**Phase 3: Add world variety (Ch 10, 11, 12, 13)**

These add content to the world around you:

- **Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Security** (Ch 9): Upgrade your killbox with embrasures, trenches, and manned turrets.
- **Vanilla Vehicles Expanded** (Ch 10): Build your first vehicle when you have fabrication and chemfuel. A jeep or truck transforms caravan speed.
- **VFE - Mechanoids** (Ch 11): This faction appears on the world map. Scout their bases before engaging.
- **Vanilla Storytellers Expanded** (Ch 11): Try Perry Persistent for more action or Winston Waves for a completely different game mode.
- **Hospitality** (Ch 13): Build a guest wing (2-3 bedrooms with guest beds, a small shopping area with shelves). Guests start arriving on the next trader visit. The silver from shopping is a nice income stream; the faction relationship bonus is the real prize.

**Phase 4: Add creatures and breeding (Ch 12, 14)**

- **Vanilla Animals Expanded** + **Alpha Animals** + **Megafauna**: New creatures appear on newly-generated maps. Send a hunting party or tame the interesting ones.
- **Giddy-Up 2** (Ch 12): Assign your handler's favourite muffalo as a personal mount. Mounted pawns travel faster and fight from horseback.
- **Vanilla Genetics Expanded** + **Alpha Genes** (Ch 14): These are late-mid-game systems. You need research capacity, a dedicated lab room, and a steady supply of components. Start with basic gene extraction and work up to hybrid animals.

## Late Game (Days 120+) — Everything Active

By now you should have all Wave 1 and Wave 2 mods active. Your colony has 12-18 colonists, advanced research, multiple vehicles, a thriving guest economy, genetics labs, and a mechanoid faction probing your defenses.

**What to focus on:**
- **Vehicle operations**: Build a garage, maintain your fleet, and use vehicles for rapid-response defence and trade caravans.
- **Genetics**: Create custom xenotypes with Alpha Genes. Breed hybrid war-beasts with Vanilla Genetics Expanded.
- **Bionics**: With A Dog Said... 2 (Ch 12) and VRE Android (Ch 14), your pawns and animals can be heavily augmented. Prioritise your front-line fighters for combat upgrades.
- **Hospitality empire**: With a well-run guest operation, you can earn steady silver and recruit talented visitors without the randomness of prisoner recruitment.
- **Faction relations**: Use the new factions and storytellers to create replayability.

**Performance considerations**: At this stage, you may notice TPS (ticks per second) dropping below the ideal 60. RocketMan (Ch 4) has built-in optimisation tools — use the RocketMan settings panel to identify heavy mods and configure animal/pathfinding limits. If things get slow, consider starting a fresh colony on a new map — the early game with all mods is dramatically different from the early game with Wave 1 only.

## Common Modded Pitfalls

1. **Load order drift.** Every time you add or remove a mod, run RimPy auto-sort. Manually dragging mods in the load order after auto-sort will cause conflicts. Let RimPy handle it.

2. **Subscribing without reading.** Always read a mod's Workshop page before subscribing. Look for version compatibility (1.6 required), required DLC, and known incompatibilities. The curated list in this guide has been verified — but if you add mods outside it, check everything.

3. **Removing mods mid-save.** Unless a mod explicitly says "safe to remove mid-save," assume it's not. Mods embed data in your save file — removing them causes errors, missing buildings, broken pawns, and save corruption. When in doubt, start a new colony.

4. **Adding too much at once.** The two-wave system exists for a reason. If you install all 100+ mods and launch a colony, you'll be overwhelmed by new items, mechanics, creatures, and events all at once. Add mods progressively. The Wave 1 → Wave 2 → mid-game progression in this chapter gives you time to learn each system before the next one arrives.

5. **The performance death spiral.** Too many animals + too many colonists + too many items on the ground + too many modded buildings = single-digit TPS. RocketMan helps, but it can't work miracles. If your TPS drops below 30, consider: culling excess animals, cleaning up item clutter, reducing colonist count, or starting a new colony.

6. **Decision paralysis.** With 100+ mods, there are always more things to build, research, craft, and explore. Pick one goal per session (build a vehicle, set up genetics, expand the hospital) and focus on it. The mods will still be there tomorrow.
