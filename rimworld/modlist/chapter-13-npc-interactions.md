# Chapter 13: NPC Interactions 🔵 Wave 2 — Intermediate

## Learning Objectives
- Increase depth of faction relationships and diplomacy
- Expand quest variety and storytelling possibilities
- Make the world feel more alive with NPC interactions

## Overview

Vanilla RimWorld's faction system is functional but shallow — visitors arrive, they trade, they leave. The mods in this chapter deepen NPC interactions, turning faction visits into dynamic social events. Hospitality is the standout: it transforms visiting traders into guests you can host, entertain, befriend, and recruit from.

These are **Wave 2 mods** — especially Hospitality, which adds a whole new colony management layer (guest rooms, shopping districts, reputation systems). Install after you have a stable mid-game colony with spare beds and resources.

## The Mods

### [More Creepjoiners](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3434682604)

- **What it does:** Adds 8+ new creepjoiner types to the Anomaly DLC. Includes the Tree Devourer (eat special trees for stacking buffs), Void Touched (craft indestructible voidmetal walls, transmute bioferrite limbs), and Highlander (steal abilities from other creepjoiners, killing them in the process). Each has unique abilities, drawbacks, and research projects.
- **Why it's here:** Vanilla creepjoiners get repetitive fast — you see the same few types every playthrough. More Creepjoiners makes Anomaly encounters feel fresh by introducing genuinely different joiners with real trade-offs. The Void Touched's voidmetal walls are a meaningful defensive tool without being a win button because they require Anomaly research first.
- **Not OP because:** Every creepjoiner has clear downsides. The Tree Devourer sacrifices trees your colony might need. The Highlander must kill other creepjoiners to steal their powers (losing their benefits permanently). Voidmetal walls require significant research investment and bioferrite. Requires Anomaly DLC.
- **Install:** OK to install now. Safe to add mid-save.

### [Hospitality (Continued)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3509486825)

- **What it does:** Turns visiting NPCs into actual guests — build guest bedrooms, assign them to beds, set up a shopping area where guests browse and buy your surplus goods, and keep them entertained with joy activities. Happy guests improve faction relations, help with chores, and sometimes agree to join your colony. Use the Comms Console to invite specific factions. Includes a dedicated guest management tab.
- **Why it's here:** In vanilla, visiting factions exist to trade — you open the shop window, buy/sell, and they leave. Hospitality turns these visits into a real social and economic system. Should you build a hotel wing? What goods should stock your shop? Should you recruit that charming visitor with great stats, or keep the faction relationship bonus? The guest tab shows exactly what each visitor needs — beds, food, joy — so you can optimise for happiness and reap the rewards. It's the most popular NPC interaction mod for a reason.
- **Not OP because:** Guests require actual investment — bedrooms, beds, food, and joy items. Poor accommodation makes guests unhappy, damaging faction relations instead of improving them. Guests can be picky (some want private rooms, not barracks). Recruiting a guest costs relationship points with their faction. Shopping works with your existing stockpile — you need surplus goods to sell. Cannot be removed from saves easily.
- **Install:** **Wave 2 mod.** Wait until you have a self-sufficient base with spare bedrooms, a steady food surplus, and a comms console. Safe to add mid-save — guests start arriving on the next visit. Requires Harmony. Load as late as possible in the load order (after Vanilla Expanded Framework). Does not work on gravships (Odyssey DLC space travel).
