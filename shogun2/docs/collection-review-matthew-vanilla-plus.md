## Philosophy Match

The collection's stated goal is *"focus of simply updating the Vanilla game without any bigger changes."* This aligns well with Tenka Fubu's "no major overhauls" principle. The collection is a Vanilla+ approach — fixes, visual enhancements, QoL. All 49 items are tested to work together.

---

## Critical Finding: Updated Versions of Our Accepted Mods

Two mods we accepted have **newer versions** in this collection:

| Our Mod (ID) | Collection's Version (ID) | Improvements |
|---|---|---|
| 12 Turns per Year (141938812) | **3357610873** by Ravonator | Adds Otomo DLC fix, fixes Shogun requirement (12 rounds instead of 4) |
| Extended Camera Minimod (99369335) | **3339446174** by Gacsam | "Updated" version — fixes 13-year-old crash issues |

**Recommendation:** Replace our current 12 Turns (141938812) with the Ravonator version (3357610873). Replace Extended Camera (99369335) with the Gacsam version (3339446174).

There's also a 2025 Bug Fix Bundle replacing the unmaintained Unofficial Shogun 2 Patch Lite (3354635861). Needs further investigation — if it supersedes Tricosis's Unofficial Patch (1795683029), we should evaluate it.

## Category-by-Category Assessment

### Clearly Good (match Tenka Fubu pillars):
- **Updated 12 Turns per Year** (3357610873) — replaces our old version
- **Updated Extended Camera** (3339446174) — replaces our old version
- **Icons Fix** (2844242500) — pure fix, Wave 0
- **Better Naval Autoresolve** (3407913145) — fix, Wave 0
- **Better Ambush Deployment** (1197036915) — QoL, Wave 0-1
- **Campaign Unit Stealth** (121311886) — vanilla-appropriate, Wave 1
- **Bullgod's Horse Texture** (2907654155) — pure graphics, Wave 1
- **Tercos Variety** (1751175580) — pure graphics, Wave 1
- **Zn Projectile Trails** (3099572218) — pure graphics, Wave 1
- **Castle Campaign Map Models** — pure graphics, Wave 1
- **Main Menu Replacer** (201235883) — cosmetic/QoL, Wave 0-1
- **Advisor Voicelines** (3122804019) — immersion, Wave 0-1

### Borderline (need discussion):
- **Research Rebalance** (2833740204) — speeds up research. Similar issue to rejected mods. Could argue it's minor enough.
- **Fire Arrows for All Ships** (1948921905) — naval balance change. Makes battles more lethal.
- **Climbing Wall Fall Reduced** (2877127183) — siege offense buff. Player advantage.
- **Lower Agent Cap of 3** (2794130173) — restricts agent spam (good) but changes game balance.
- **Blacksmith Branching** (1747322372) — changes building choices meaningfully.
- **Semi Random AI** (495479150) — could pair with or replace UAI.
- **Lennys Autoresolve Rebalance** (3301645442) — more aggressive rebalance than Better Naval Autoresolve.

### Redundant (if UAI used):
- Semi Random AI is AI personality randomization, UAI is CAI behavior. They touch different systems and are technically compatible, but having both might make AI unpredictable in undesired ways.

## Recommendations

1. **Immediately swap**: Replace 141938812 → 3357610873 (12 Turns with Otomo fix) and 99369335 → 3339446174 (Extended Camera updated)
2. **Strongly consider adding**: Icons Fix (2844242500), Better Naval Autoresolve (3407913145), Better Ambush Deployment (1197036915), Bullgod's Horse (2907654155)
3. **Evaluate the 2025 Bug Fix Bundle** as replacement for Unofficial Shogun 2 Patch
4. **Discuss borderline mods**: Agent Cap, Research Rebalance, Fire Arrows, Climbing Fall — these are the ones that could violate "no overpower" rules
5. **Skip for now**: Semi Random AI (UAI is enough), castle campaign map models (5+ mods for a minor visual is bloat), Zn Projectile Trails (personal preference)
