## 2. "Shogun 2 Must Have" — 3422983577

**Type:** Collection (1 item)
**Verdict:** Single mod only — easy call.

Contains one mod: **"Double zone of control" (3287258494)**
- Increases naval zone of control by 100%
- Makes it easier to create naval choke points and catch enemy fleets
- Must be first in load order

### Assessment
This is a **balance change**, not a fix. Naval ZoC affects how easily you can trap enemy fleets vs. them slipping through your blockade. Doubling it:
- Makes naval defense significantly easier (player advantage — AI can't optimize choke points as well)
- Reduces the "chase single ships around" frustration (QoL benefit)
- Changes naval campaign dynamics meaningfully

**Recommendation:** BORDERLINE. The QoL argument (less frustrating naval micro) is valid, but doubling ZoC is a substantial balance change. If desired, it belongs in Wave 2 (mechanics). Not recommended for Wave 0-1.
