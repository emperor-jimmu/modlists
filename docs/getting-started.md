# Getting Started with Stellaris

> **Guide:** This section teaches you Stellaris from scratch. If you've never launched the game, start here.

## What is Stellaris?

Stellaris is a grand strategy game by Paradox Interactive where you lead a spacefaring civilization from the dawn of spaceflight to galactic domination — through exploration, diplomacy, war, and discovery.

## Core Concepts

### Your Empire

You start with a single planet and must expand, manage resources, research technology, and navigate the galaxy.

### Resources

- **Energy Credits** — currency for upkeep and purchases
- **Minerals** — raw materials for buildings and ships
- **Food** — feeds your population
- ** alloys** — advanced materials for warships and megastructures
- **Consumer Goods** — keeps your people happy and science running
- **Unity** — cultural progress, unlocks traditions
- **Influence** — political capital for diplomacy, claims, and edicts
- **Research** — three types (Physics, Society, Engineering) that unlock technology

### Basic Flow

1. **Explore** — send science ships to survey nearby systems
2. **Expand** — build starbases and colonize new worlds
3. **Exploit** — develop your planets with districts and buildings
4. **Exterminate** — defend yourself and project power

## Galaxy Setup

When starting a new game, you'll configure:

- **Galaxy Size** — Small (400-600 stars) for beginners, Medium (600-800) for experienced
- **Galaxy Shape** — Spiral, Elliptical, or Ring
- **AI Empires** — 4-8 recommended for a first game
- **Difficulty** — Civilian or Ensign for beginners
- **Crisis Difficulty** — 1x (default) for first playthrough
- **Mid-Game Start Year** — 2300
- **End-Game Start Year** — 2400
- **Victory Year** — 2500
