# Monsters and Combat

Quality-of-life improvements and visual tweaks for monster encounters.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| Mini Bars | Adds customizable health bars above monsters. Shows remaining HP at a glance; supports different bar themes (mini/long), range-limiting, and toggle for showing only damaged enemies. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/8329) — SMAPI-based by GukelZ, 9.5k+ endorsements. Configurable via Generic Mod Config Menu or in-game commands. Compatible with Ridgeside Village and Swim mods. Updated for 1.6. | Below foundations |

## Notes

- **Mini Bars** is purely informational — it does not change monster stats or behaviour.
