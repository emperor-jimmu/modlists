## Notes

- **Yet Another Harvest With Scythe Mod** requires **StardewHack** (a library mod from the same author). Configure which crops are scythe-harvestable vs hand-pick-only in GMCM. Known incompatibility with Tractor Mod.
- **Immersive Sprinklers and Scarecrows** replaces both the older Immersive Sprinklers and Immersive Scarecrows mods — objects placed with the old mods won't carry over. Saves objects in location data so hoe dirt removal won't break them.
- **Em's Hedgehogs** only need a regular coop — no extra buildings required. The fruit they gather is seasonal and varies by what's available.
- **Em's Cows** is a visual alt-textures pack first, but also serves as the dependency for Em and Harry's Exotic Cattle which turns rare breeds into separate gameplay-relevant animals.
- Install in order: Em's Cows → Em and Harry's Exotic Cattle. Hedgehogs are independent and can be installed at any time.
- **Dunleigh Farm (Foragable Area)** adds a small seasonal foraging patch to your farm. The Central Station dependency is optional — only needed if you want warp access to the area.
- **Cornucopia** mods form a cohesive suite: More Crops → More Flowers → Artisan Machines → Cooking Recipes. Trees Retextured (in Graphics Base) is a visual companion. All are independently optional but designed to work together.
- **Wildflour's Atelier Goods** and **Cornucopia - Artisan Machines** both add artisan machines with different focuses — WAG covers baker/brewer/candy/frozen/boutique; Cornucopia covers dehydrator/smoker/wax barrel/vinegar keg/etc. They coexist without conflicts.
- **Warm Stalls and Cozy Boxes** modifies barn and coop interiors — it may conflict with other mods that edit the same interior maps. It is compatible with SVE, Walk of Life, Vanilla Plus Professions, and many barn/coop size mods listed on its mod page.
- **Em's River Wildlife** and **Em's Farm Goose and Geese** are from the same author (RivalRabbit) as Em's Hedgehogs, Em's Cows, and Em and Harry's Exotic Cattle. All are independent and can be installed in any order.
