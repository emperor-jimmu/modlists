# Farming and Making

New farm animals, machines, and crafting content that expand the farming experience.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| Em's Hedgehogs | Adds hedgehogs as a new coop animal. Hedgehogs need grass and a safe coop to sleep; every other morning they leave a piece of gathered fruit (plum, apricot, blackberry, or salmonberry). | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/41595) — CP-based. Requires Content Patcher. Simple, cute addition — no new buildings or items needed. | After foundations, alongside other animal content mods |
| Em's Cows | Adds 40+ real-life cow breed skins as Alternative Textures options for your barn cows. Choose from Holstein, Angus, Jersey, Highland, Belted Galloway, and many more. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/34291) — CP + AT-based, 1.6 Compatible. Requires Content Patcher, Alternative Textures, and SMAPI. Parent mod for Em and Harry's Exotic Cattle. | Above animal content mods that reference these breeds |
| Em and Harry's Exotic Cattle | Turns the rarer cow breeds from Em's Cows into distinct barn animals with their own produce. If you love Em's Cows but want exotic breeds to feel special, this mod gives them unique products and behaviour. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/41502) — CP-based, 1.6 Compatible. Requires Em's Cows and Content Patcher. Compatible with Alternative Textures. | After Em's Cows |

## Notes

- **Em's Hedgehogs** only need a regular coop — no extra buildings required. The fruit they gather is seasonal and varies by what's available.
- **Em's Cows** is a visual alt-textures pack first, but also serves as the dependency for Em and Harry's Exotic Cattle which turns rare breeds into separate gameplay-relevant animals.
- Install in order: Em's Cows → Em and Harry's Exotic Cattle. Hedgehogs are independent and can be installed at any time.
