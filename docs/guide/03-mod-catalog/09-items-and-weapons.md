# Items and Weapons

Visual retextures for items — rings, artifacts, minerals, fish, crops, artisan goods, food, and special items. These are purely cosmetic and do not change gameplay behaviour.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| Better Rings | Retextures all ring sprites with cleaner, more detailed designs. Each ring type gets a distinct look. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/8642) — CP-based by BBR, 1.7k+ endorsements. Pure visual replacement — no gameplay changes. Requires CP. | Above other item retextures |
| Fancy Artifacts Retexture | Retextures all artifact sprites — ancient dolls, skeletal remains, lost books, ornamental fans, and more. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/16044) — CP-based by Aimon111, 4k+ endorsements. Keeps artifacts visually distinct and recognisable. Requires CP. | Above mineral retextures |
| DSHi Minerals Retexture | Retextures all gemstones, minerals, and geode-processed items — diamonds, prismatic shards, jade, frozen tears, and every geode mineral. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/18725) — CP-based by DSHi. Part of the DSHi item retexture series. All DSHi mods share a consistent visual style. | After artifacts |
| DSHi Fish Retexture | Retextures all fish, trash, bait, and tackle items — every catchable fish, algae, seaweed, trash, and fishing equipment. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/21465) — CP-based by DSHi. Companion to the other DSHi retextures for a unified item look. | After minerals |
| DSHi Shipping Items Retexture | Retextures crops, artisan goods, and forage — fruits, vegetables, flowers, wines, jellies, cheeses, and wild forageables. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/18769) — CP-based by DSHi. Covers the most commonly shipped item categories. | After fish retextures |
| DSHi Food Retexture | Retextures concessions, desert festival foods, cooking ingredients, and prepared meals. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/18726) — CP-based by DSHi. Pairs with the DSHi Shipping Items set for full item coverage. | After shipping retextures |
| DSHi Special Items Retexture | Retextures quest items, totems, wallet items, trinkets, and fertilizers. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/32206) — CP-based by DSHi. Covers the remaining item categories not included in other DSHi packs. | After food retextures |

## Notes

- All items in this section are **purely cosmetic** — they replace sprite textures only and do not affect stats, recipes, or game mechanics.
- The **DSHi series** (5 mods) is designed as a cohesive set. Install all of them for a consistent visual style across every item category, or pick individual packs for specific item types. Load them in the order listed above.
- **Better Rings** can be used alongside the DSHi mods — they change different item categories.
- No Content Patcher conflicts between these mods — each targets distinct sprite sheets.
