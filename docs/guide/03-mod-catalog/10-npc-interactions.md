# NPC Interactions

New NPCs, dialogue expansions, and social interaction enhancements.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| Tilly the Tea-Loving Librarian | Adds a new marriageable NPC to Pelican Town — Tilly, a tea-loving bookworm who abandoned her failed business in Zuzu City. Includes custom location, heart events, dialogue, and schedule. Supports custom portraits (Nyapu-style optional). | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/38008) — CP + SMAPI-based, v2.7.5, 784+ endorsements. Requires Content Patcher, BETAS, and Midnight's Misc Tilesheets. GMCM recommended for Nyapu-style portrait and baby appearance configuration. | After foundations, alongside other NPC mods |

## Notes

- **Tilly** is a full custom NPC with marriage support, heart events, and a custom location. Install all required dependencies (BETAS, Midnight's Misc Tilesheets) before adding this mod.
- Only one portrait mod per NPC can be active at a time. Tilly includes optional Nyapu-style portrait support toggleable via GMCM.
