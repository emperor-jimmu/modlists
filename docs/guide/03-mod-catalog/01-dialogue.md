# Dialogue

Mods that expand NPC dialogue, adding more variety and depth to character interactions.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| Canon-Friendly Dialogue Expansion | Adds ~4,200 new lines of dialogue across all 34 heart-level NPCs — day-to-day, festivals, marriage, and loved-gift responses. Canon-esque, SFW, family-friendly tone. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/2544) — CP-based, v3.0.9, 60k+ endorsements. Uses 50/50 compat system alongside other dialogue mods (Haywrite's, Unique Gift Dialogues, More Specific Gift Reactions). Per-NPC config via GMCM. i18n translation support. | After foundations, alongside NPC mods |

## Notes

- Compatible with Gender Neutrality Mod Tokens, Diverse Stardew Valley, Haywrite's Dialogue Expansion, and More Specific Gift Reactions — all auto-detected.
- Year 1-2 festival dialogue matches vanilla; years 3+ have new sets; year 5+ randomises between all sets.
- Requires SMAPI, Content Patcher, and optionally GMCM for in-game configuration.
