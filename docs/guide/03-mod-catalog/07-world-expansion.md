## Notes

- **East Scarp** is one of the largest expansion mods alongside SVE and Ridgeside Village. It is designed to be compatible with both. The many dependencies are required for its custom maps, NPCs, and mechanics.
- **Stardew Aquarium** acts as a fish-donation counterpart to the vanilla Museum. The collection menu tracks which fish you've donated. Legendary Bait lets you re-catch legendary fish for display.
- **Desert Oasis** changes the desert map tilesheet — use with terrain recolours that support the desert. Does not change gameplay or add new NPCs.
- All mods in this section require **Central Station** for warp-travel integration. Install Central Station first (see Foundations).
- **Lilybrook** is a full village expansion accessible via a bus ticket purchase. It adds significant content — crops, fish, forageables, recipes, and 17 NPCs. Compatible with SVE and East Scarp. Consider adding after completing the vanilla main content to avoid being overwhelmed.
- **Crystal Island** overhauls Ginger Island's visuals and theme — it is a late-game area so there is no early-game overwhelm. Compatible with SVE, GIEL-R, Buildable Ginger Island, and Lnh's Overhaul. Highly configurable with options for witches, seasons, crops, and palette themes.
