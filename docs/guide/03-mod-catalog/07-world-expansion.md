# World Expansion

Mods that add new areas, locations, and significant content expansions to the world.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| East Scarp | A major world expansion adding a new area east of the mountain — new NPCs, shops, forage, fish, and hidden secrets. Modular design with optional add-on NPCs. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5787) — CP + SMAPI-based, v3.0.9, 19k+ endorsements. Requires CP, FTM, SpaceCore, MEEP, HxW Tilesheets, and Lumisteria Tilesheets (indoor + outdoor). Compatible with SVE and Ridgeside Village. | After foundations, above terrain recolours |
| Stardew Aquarium | A fish collection museum — catch and donate fish to populate aquarium tanks across multiple rooms. Includes a collection menu, legendary bait for recatching legendaries, and custom NPC tourists. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/6372) — CP + SMAPI-based, v2.3.0, 6.2k+ endorsements. Requires Central Station, CP, Custom Companions, Custom NPC Exclusions, EMP, HxW Tilesheets, SpaceCore. Compatible with 1.6. | After foundations (after Central Station) |
| (CP) Desert Oasis | Transforms the desert into a lush oasis with palm trees, water features, and new forageable items. Changes the visual theme of the desert area. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/31978) — CP-based, 1.6 Compatible. Requires Central Station, CP, FTM. Adds spawnable items via FTM. | After terrain recolours, same as other location overhauls |

## Notes

- **East Scarp** is one of the largest expansion mods alongside SVE and Ridgeside Village. It is designed to be compatible with both. The many dependencies are required for its custom maps, NPCs, and mechanics.
- **Stardew Aquarium** acts as a fish-donation counterpart to the vanilla Museum. The collection menu tracks which fish you've donated. Legendary Bait lets you re-catch legendary fish for display.
- **Desert Oasis** changes the desert map tilesheet — use with terrain recolours that support the desert. Does not change gameplay or add new NPCs.
- All mods in this section require **Central Station** for warp-travel integration. Install Central Station first (see Foundations).
