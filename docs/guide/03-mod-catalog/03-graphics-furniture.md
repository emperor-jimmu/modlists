## Notes

- **Furniture mods** that add new items require their respective frameworks (Calcifer for Green Grove, Furniture Framework for Retro Style). Nostalgic Old Furniture and Myc's Seasonal Fireplace work with CP alone.
- Most furniture mods add their own catalogue purchasable from Robin's shop. Items can also be spawned via CJB Item Spawner if preferred.
- **HxW Greenhouse Furniture** and **HxW Outdoor Furniture** add new furniture items (CP-based). Their Muski Recolor packs provide AT-based texture alternatives applied via Paint Bucket.
