# Graphics Portraits

Portrait replacements that change how NPCs look in dialogue and menu screens.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| mono's Portraits | A full portrait replacement for all vanilla villagers and marriage candidates, drawn at native scale with a faithful but distinct style. Includes winter outfits for 1.6. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/16649) — CP-based, updated for 1.6. Does not cover modded NPCs. | Anywhere in graphics section |

## Notes

- Only one portrait mod can be active at a time per NPC. Choose one set that fits your preferred art style.
- Seasonal outfit variants (like mono's winter outfits) require the mod's config to be set correctly.
