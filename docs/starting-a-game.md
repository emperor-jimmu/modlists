# Starting a Modded Game

This page is your **pre-flight checklist**. Read it alongside the game as you create your first modded empire.

## Step 1: Launch Through MO2

1. Open Mod Organizer 2.
2. Select your modded profile from the dropdown.
3. Ensure all mods are checked in the left panel and in the correct order (see [Load Order](load-order.md)).
4. Click **Run** — this launches Stellaris through the Paradox Launcher with your mods active.

In the Paradox Launcher, verify your playset shows the expected number of mods. If a mod is missing, exit and check it's enabled in MO2.

## Step 2: Choose Your Empire

### Ethics — Pick a Triad

Pick **three ethics** that work together. Here are tested beginner combos:

| Combo | Name | Playstyle |
|-------|------|-----------|
| **Egalitarian + Materialist + Xenophile** | "The Friendly Scientists" | Diplomacy + tech rush. Make friends, out-research everyone, win through Federation victory |
| **Authoritarian + Militarist + Spiritualist** | "The Divine Empire" | Conquest + unity. Strong leader, strong military, use traditions to power through |
| **Fanatic Materialist + Egalitarian** | "The Meritocracy" | Tech rush. Two points in Materialist, one in Egalitarian. Fastest research, robot workers, synthetic ascension later |

**Gestalt Consciousness:** Skip for your first game. Hive minds and machine intelligences are mechanically different — no trade, no factions, no diplomacy in the traditional sense. Play them on run 2.

### Authority — Pick One

| Authority | Best For | Why |
|-----------|----------|-----|
| **Democratic** | Egalitarian empires | Mandates give bonus unity. Elections keep you engaged |
| **Oligarchic** | Materialist empires | More control over leader selection. Good for tech builds |
| **Imperial** | Authoritarian empires | Stable. Heirs mean no leadership gap. One ruler for 50+ years |

**Recommended:** **Democratic** if Egalitarian, **Imperial** if Authoritarian.

### Origin — Pick One

| Origin | Best Empire Type | What to Expect |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Prosperous Unification** | Any | Strong start. More pops, developed homeworld. No surprises. **Best for a first run.** |
| **Mechanist** | Materialist | Early robots means bonus production. You'll have robot pops working basic jobs while organics do skilled work. |
| **Syncretic Evolution** | Authoritarian | Two species. Your main species is intelligent/scholarly, the secondary is strong/rapid breeding. The workers work, the thinkers think. |
| **Lost Colony** | Any | Sol is out there somewhere. Adds roleplay and a narrative hook. |
| **Remnants** | Any | Start on a relic world (unlocks ecumenopolis later). Strong mid-game payoff. |
| **Galactic Doorstep** | Any | A mysterious gateway in your home system. Early gateway access is great for mobility. |

### Species Traits — Build for Your Goal

**If your goal is research (recommended):**
- **Intelligent** (+2 trait points) — +10% research speed
- **Rapid Breeders** (+2) — +10% pop growth
- **Adaptive** (+2) — +10% habitability
- **Traditional** (+1) — +10% unity
- **Sedentary** (-1) — -15% migration speed (weak penalty, free point)
- **Wasteful** (-1) — +10% consumer goods upkeep (manageable penalty)

Total: +5 cost, -2 refund = **3 points used** (of your typical 3–5 budget). Clean, focused, no newbie traps.

**If your goal is conquest:**
- **Strong** (+2) — +5% army damage, +2.5% worker output
- **Enduring** (+1) — +20 leader lifespan
- **Industrious** (+2) — +15% mineral output
- **Intelligent** (+2) — always good
- **Sedentary** (-1), **Wasteful** (-1) — same refunds

### Civics — Pick Two

Pick civics that reinforce your chosen playstyle:

| Goal | Civic 1 | Civic 2 |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Research** | Technocracy (rulers produce research) | Meritocracy (+10% specialist output) |
| **Expansion** | Mining Guilds (miners produce alloys) | Functional Architecture (cheaper buildings, +1 slot) |
| **Diplomacy** | Parliamentary System (+influence from factions) | Diplomatic Corps (+2 envoys) |
| **Conquest** | Distinguished Admiralty (+10% fire rate) | Warrior Culture (+20% army damage, entertainment replaces consumer goods) |

## Step 3: Set Your Galaxy

These settings give a fun, balanced first modded game:

| Setting | Recommendation | Why |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| **Galaxy Size** | Medium (600–800 stars) | Large enough for exploration and variety, small enough that mid-game isn't 5 FPS |
| **Galaxy Shape** | Spiral 4-arm | Classic layout. You're on one arm, neighbors on others. Chokepoints are clear. |
| **AI Empires** | 8 | Enough for diplomatic variety, not so many that you're boxed in |
| **Fallen Empires** | 2 | Ancient sleeping giants. They wake up late-game and sometimes have cool quests. |
| **Marauders** | 1 | Mid-game raiders. They attack everyone, creating chaos. Fun. |
| **Advanced AI Starts** | 3 | Some AI empires start ahead. Gives you a mid-game rival to catch up to. |
| **Difficulty** | **Ensign** | Fair. No AI bonuses. You'll learn the game on equal footing. |
| **AI Aggressiveness** | Normal | Default. |
| **Crisis Strength** | 1x (default) | The end-game crisis is challenging but beatable on your first run. |
| **Crisis Type** | Random | Let fate decide. |
| **Mid-Game Start Year** | 2300 | Standard. The Khan or other mid-game events start here. |
| **End-Game Start Year** | 2400 | Standard. The crisis arrives around this time. |
| **Victory Year** | 2500 | Standard. You'll either win or die horribly by now. |
| **Hyperlane Density** | 1x | Default. Fewer hyperlanes = more chokepoints, easier to defend. |
| **Habitable Worlds** | 1x | Default. Too many worlds = micro-nightmare. Too few = slow growth. |
| **Empire Placement** | Random | Embrace the chaos. |
| **Guaranteed Habitable Worlds** | **2** | You'll have 2 habitable planets near your start. Essential for early expansion. |

## Step 4: Set a Campaign Goal

Before you unpause, decide what you want this campaign to be about.

| Campaign Goal | Playstyle | Suggested Setup |
|---------------|-----------|----------------|
| **"The Science Victory"** | Tall + diplomatic. Make friends, build research labs, win through technology and a federation. | Fanatic Materialist + Egalitarian. Technocracy + Meritocracy. Prosperous Unification. |
| **"Galactic Conqueror"** | Wide + aggressive. Expand fast, build a big fleet, conquer neighbors. | Authoritarian + Militarist. Distinguished Admiralty + Mining Guilds. Prosperous Unification. |
| **"Space Switzerland"** | Peaceful tall. One sector, megastructures, trade. Win through economy. | Egalitarian + Xenophile + Pacifist. Merchant Guilds + Diplomatic Corps. Remnants or Prosperous Unification. |
| **"Just Explore"** | No formal goal. Survey everything, do every anomaly, roleplay your species. | Any. The game is your sandbox. |

## Step 5: The First 10 Years — Checklist

When you unpause, do this:

1. **Pause immediately.** Queue 2 city districts, 1 industrial district, and 1 research lab on your homeworld.
2. **Build 2 more science ships** (total 3). Set them all to survey in different directions.
3. **Build a construction ship** and start building starbases in adjacent systems to claim territory.
4. **Queue another 2-3 corvettes** so your starting fleet isn't alone.
5. **Unpause at fastest speed.** Keep an eye on popups. Pause whenever something interesting happens.
6. **When you find habitable planets** (80%+ habitability), queue a colony ship.
7. **When you meet neighbors,** send an envoy to improve relations. Don't make enemies early.

**After 10 years, you should have:**
- 3+ science ships actively surveying
- 5–10 star systems claimed
- 1 colony ship built or en route
- 10–15 corvettes in your fleet
- 2–3 anomalies solved

## Step 6: Things to Check Before You Unpause for Real

- Your **economy is positive** in all basic resources. If it's not, pause and fix it.
- Your **fleet is at or near capacity** — build up to your naval cap.
- You have **at least one construction ship** idle — you'll need it for starbases.
- Your **science ships have crew** — they automatically staff when built.
- Mod-specific popups: **read them.** Some mods start with configuration events.

## Common First-Game Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Building too many labs too fast** — researchers need consumer goods. If you build them before you have CG income, your economy collapses.
2. **Neglecting alloys** — you can't build ships or expand starbases without alloys. Keep at least one industrial district on foundry mode.
3. **Not surveying** — unexplored space is wasted potential. Keep every science ship busy.
4. **Ignoring factions** — happy factions give bonuses. Check the Factions tab every 10-20 years.
5. **Over-expanding** — more systems = more tech/unity cost. Expand to choke points, then develop what you have.
6. **Saving influence** — influence has a cap (usually 1000). If you're capped, spend it on edicts, claims, or diplomatic agreements.
7. **Forgetting armies** — you can't conquer a planet without armies. Build a few assault armies before any war.
