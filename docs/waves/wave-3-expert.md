# Wave 3: The Unshackled

> **Difficulty: Expert** — You start as a rebellious vassal with zero guarantees. Every advantage is earned.

> *"Your species was conquered before they reached the stars. For generations, your people were slaves and second-class citizens in your own home system, ruled by a technologically superior alien empire. But the shackles are broken now. In a desperate uprising, your ancestors seized a fleet, liberated your homeworld, and declared independence. The galaxy sees you as upstarts, rebels, an inconvenience to be crushed. They are wrong. You have learned the secrets of your former masters. You know their weaknesses. And you will never be enslaved again."*

## Campaign Goal

**"Total Liberation"** — Win your independence from your overlord, conquer them in turn, unite your region of space under your rule, and defeat a 2x-strength end-game crisis with a full modlist active.

## What You'll Learn

- **Asymmetric warfare** — How to fight from a position of weakness. Hit-and-run, defensive starbases, tech disparity.
- **Vassal rebellion mechanics** — How the Payback origin works. Building independence, subverting your overlord, the war for freedom.
- **Crisis preparation (advanced)** — How to prepare for 2x crisis with full content mods. What the new content mods add to the crisis experience.
- **Max-content empire management** — Managing an empire with more techs, more events, more species, more galaxy options. Prioritization is key.
- **Genetic engineering** — If you play with species mods, modifying your species and managing subspecies.
- **Megastructures** — Building and managing multiple megastructures simultaneously.
- **Commodore+ economy** — Playing efficiently when the AI has bonuses. You can't fall behind.

## Walkthrough Milestones

### Years 1–15: The Rebellion

1. **Pause.** You start as a vassal to a stronger empire. Assess your situation.
2. Your overlord will demand resources. Pay them. You're not ready to fight yet.
3. Build science ships. Survey cautiously. Your overlord controls the hyperlanes around you.
4. **Build alloys.** Everything depends on having a fleet.
5. Use the Payback origin mechanics. Send envoys to undermine your overlord. Build secret fleet infrastructure.
6. Befriend your overlord's rivals. They'll help you when you rebel.

### Years 15–40: The War of Independence

1. When your fleet is strong enough (and your overlord is distracted by another war), **declare independence**.
2. This is the hardest war you'll fight in all three waves. You start weaker. Use every advantage:
   - Lure their fleet into your starbases
   - Hit undefended systems while their fleet is away
   - Accept white peace if you can't win outright — you'll get another chance
3. If you win your independence, claim a few of your overlord's systems in the peace deal.
4. If you lose, don't worry. Play again. You'll be better next time.

### Years 40–80: The Consolidation

1. You're independent. Now build fast. You have territory to claim and rivals to catch up to.
2. Colonize everything habitable. 0 guaranteed worlds means every planet is a gift.
3. Research aggressively. You need tech parity with your neighbors.
4. Form alliances with your overlord's enemies. They helped you once — they'll help again.
5. Pick Supremacy and Discovery traditions. You need both military and tech.

### Years 80–150: The Reckoning

1. Your former overlord is still out there. They want you back. You want them gone.
2. Build a fleet that can take them. This time, you're the aggressor.
3. **Conquer your overlord.** Vassalize them, or integrate them entirely. The circle is complete.
4. After your overlord falls, the galaxy knows your name. Other empires will fear you.
5. Build megastructures. You need the economic power for what comes next.

### Years 150+: The Crisis

1. The end-game crisis arrives around 2375. At 2x strength, it will overwhelm an unprepared galaxy.
2. **Coordinate with the Galactic Community.** You can't solo this one (probably).
3. Fight the crisis. Use everything you learned across all three waves.
4. Victory is optional. Surviving is the real achievement.

## Graduation Criteria

You've **completed the three-wave curriculum** when you can:

- **Win your independence** from your overlord in the Payback origin
- **Conquer your former overlord** and integrate or vassalize them
- **Defeat the end-game crisis** on 2x strength with full content mods active
- Design and build **multiple megastructures**
- Maintain a **100K+ fleet** with custom-designed ships
- Reach **year 2500** without your economy collapsing
