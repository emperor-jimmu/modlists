# Wave 2: The Eternal Legion

> **Difficulty: Intermediate** — You know the basics. Now learn war, conquest, and how to manage an empire built on strength.

> *"Your people are the inheritors of a long-dead empire whose relic cities still pierce the sky of your homeworld. The spires of the Ancients are a constant reminder of what was lost — and what could be reclaimed. Your society is built on a single creed: strength is the measure of worth. The weak serve, the strong lead, and the galaxy belongs to those who take it. The Ecumenopolis that sleeps beneath your feet whispers of a glory you were born to restore."*

## Campaign Goal

**"Galactic Conqueror"** — Expand aggressively, build a dominant fleet, conquer your neighbors, and establish a vassal empire. The relic world beneath your feet is a reminder: you were born to rule.

## What You'll Learn

- **Fleet combat** — Ship roles (corvette screen, destroyer line, cruiser punch, battleship artillery). Countering enemy designs.
- **Ship designer** — How to customize ships. Why you shouldn't use auto-design.
- **War exhaustion** — How claims work, war goals, status quo peace, when to push vs when to settle.
- **Vassals** — How to create, manage, and integrate vassals. Tributaries, bulwarks, scholariums, prospectoriums.
- **Traditions** — Which tradition trees support conquest (Supremacy, Domination, Prosperity).
- **Factions** — Keeping authoritarian factions happy. Managing slaves and stratified society.
- **Relic worlds** — Restoring your homeworld to an ecumenopolis. The ultimate economic engine.
- **The Remnants origin chain** — The archaeology site, the rewards, the lore.

## Walkthrough Milestones

### Years 1–10: Military Buildup

1. **Pause.** Your relic world has unique district options. Prioritize industrial districts for alloys.
2. Build 3 science ships. Survey for chokepoints and habitable worlds.
3. Build a construction ship. Rush to claim chokepoints.
4. **Build corvettes non-stop.** You need a fleet that deters aggression. Aim for 30 corvettes by year 10.
5. Find your guaranteed world. Colonize it immediately.

### Years 10–30: First Conquest

1. Identify your weakest neighbor. Rival them for influence.
2. Build claims on 2–3 of their systems.
3. When your fleet is stronger, **declare war**. Your first conquest should be quick.
4. Occupy their planets with armies. Build assault armies before the war.
5. Take your claims in the peace deal. Don't overreach — status quo is fine if you've taken what you wanted.

### Years 30–80: Vassal Empire

1. Your second war should aim to **vassalize**, not conquer. A vassal pays you resources without the micro.
2. Set your vassal to Prospectorium (minerals) or Bulwark (military). Both feed your war machine.
3. Keep building alloy production. Alloys = fleets = power.
4. Research the **Ecumenopolis Restoration** technology. Your relic world can become a city-world.
5. Pick Supremacy and Domination traditions.

### Years 80–150: The War Machine

1. By now you should have 3+ vassals and the strongest fleet in your neighborhood.
2. Convert your relic homeworld into an ecumenopolis. This single planet can produce more alloys than your entire previous empire.
3. Fight multi-front wars. Use vassals as buffer states.
4. When the Galactic Community forms, dominate it or ignore it. You have the fleet to back up your外交.

### Years 150+: Crisis & Legacy

1. The end-game crisis arrives around 2400. Your fleets should be 200K+ power.
2. Fight the crisis. Your militaristic empire is built for this.
3. Whether you win or lose, you've learned more than most players ever do.

## Graduation Criteria

You're ready for [Wave 3](wave-3-expert.md) when you can:

- **Conquer at least one neighbor empire** before year 2300
- **Design your own ships** (not auto-design) and win a fleet engagement
- **Manage vassals** — create, set their type, and collect resources from them
- **Restore your relic world** to an ecumenopolis
- **Maintain a fleet of 50K+ power** by year 2350
- **Win a war** against an empire with equivalent or superior fleet power (not just picking on weaker neighbors)
