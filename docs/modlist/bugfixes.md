## Installation Notes

- **Version-lock all bugfix mods** in MO2. These are the most likely to be silently broken by a game update.
- Bugfix mods should be **first in your load order** — nothing should override a bugfix.
- Mod authors typically update within 1-3 weeks of a Stellaris patch. Until then, keep your game version locked to the previous release.

## How to Add More

Browse the Steam Workshop with the `Bug Fixes` tag. When adding a bugfix mod, verify it:
1. Doesn't add new content (pure fixes only)
2. Is compatible with the current Stellaris version
3. Lists its fix changelog so you know what's being patched
4. Is maintained by an active author
