# Galaxy & Setup

> Mods that expand galaxy generation, starting options, planetary diversity, hyperlane density, and game rules.

| Mod | Source | Version-Locked | Wave | Notes |
|-----|--------|----------------|------|-------|
| [Planetary Diversity](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=819148835) | Steam Workshop | No | 3 | Adds new planet classes, habitable types, and planet modifiers. Fully compatible with 4.4.x. Works with most galaxy mods. |
| [Gigastructural Engineering & More](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1121692237) | Steam Workshop | No | 3 | 45+ megastructures, 4 optional crises to balance the power curve. Requires compatibility patches for UI Overhaul Dynamic and Real Space if used together. |
| [Guilli's Planet Modifiers and Features](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=865040033) | Steam Workshop | No | 3 | 400+ planet modifiers with custom compatibility engine. Works alongside Planetary Diversity and Gigastructures. |
| [Shrouded Regions](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3483853399) | Steam Workshop | No | 3 | Adds psionic dead zones, Galactic Crossing hyperlane network, unique origin, and midgame crisis. Actively maintained for 4.4. May conflict with mods that alter hyperlane/nebula visuals. |
| [Ideal System Locator](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3243319558) | Steam Workshop | No | 3 | Utility tool that highlights systems best suited for kilostructures and megastructures. QoL complement to Gigastructures. |

## What to Look For

- **Galaxy size** — larger galaxy options, more star types
- **Hyperlane density** — custom density options for more/less connectivity
- **Planetary diversity** — more planet types, modifiers, classes
- **Starting options** — additional game rules, sliders, galaxy shapes
- **Precursor diversity** — more precursor civilizations and backstories
