## Long War of the Chosen compatibility (user-supplied, 2026-08-12)

The collection page's compatibility notes for LWOTC:

- [WOTC] Cost-Based Ability Colors - compatible, but requires configuration edits (LWOTC has a built-in alternative)
- [WOTC] Purifier Grenades Fix - incompatible and unnecessary (LWOTC removes grenades from Purifiers)
- WotC: "Increase Combat Intelligence" for everyone - unnecessary in LWOTC
- [WOTC] Full Shadow Report - limited compatibility, requires MCM edits to avoid UI overlap
- [WOTC] One squad on new Avenger defense - integrated into LWOTC
- [WOTC] Better Armory Item Stats - integrated into LWOTC
