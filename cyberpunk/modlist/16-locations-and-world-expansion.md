rooftop access, and environmental additions that expand Night City.

### Marmur Bank (Watson District Branch)

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/22048>
- **Version:** 2.1
- **Released:** 2025-06-01
- **Summary:** Opens the Marmur Bank interior across from Tom's Diner near H10. ~25 NPCs on two time schedules (busy 6am-6pm, quieter at night). Created with World Builder.
- **Why:** Adds a detailed, populated new interior to a prominent locked building in Watson. Fills an empty space players walk past constantly. v2.0 updated for game version 2.3.
- **Conflicts:** May conflict with mods adding random shootouts in the area (e.g., Night City Alive).
- **Dependencies:** ArchiveXL
- **MO2 Notes:** Extract to game root. Also compatible with Marmur Bank - NC Bank add-on (12470) for deposit/withdraw functionality.
- **Performance:** Light (25 NPCs, single interior cell)

### Urban Garden Grocery Store

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/28520>
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Released:** 2026-03-29
- **Summary:** Adds a functional grocery store interior in Santo Domingo, overrun by drugs. Browse aisles, interact with the environment, and use as a drug cooking station when paired with Drug Dealer mod.
- **Why:** Night City lacks mundane civilian infrastructure — everyone eats from vending machines. A grocery store adds civilian world authenticity, making the city feel inhabited by normal people who buy groceries. Serves the "living world" pillar by filling in missing civic infrastructure. Note: misclassified as adult content by Nexus — this is a normal location mod.
- **Conflicts:** Any mod that changes the same building space. Compatible with Sit Anywhere and Immersive Night City Fixes.
- **Dependencies:** Phantom Liberty DLC (required), ArchiveXL (required). Drug Dealer - Become a Kingpin (optional, for functional cooking station).
- **MO2 Notes:** Extract to game root.
- **Performance:** Light (5 NPCs, single interior cell)

### Pacifica Enhanced

- **Nexus:** <https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/21848>
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Released:** 2025-11-12
- **Summary:** Adds props (trash, tents, makeshift shacks, construction debris) to empty areas of Pacifica, making the district feel lived-in and believable. Works with Patch 2.31.

- **MO2 Notes:**
