## Notes

**Wave 0 fact-check log (Task 4 Step 8, 2026-08-03, sources: wiki.factorio.com):**
- Steam power: 2.0.7 changed boilers/heat exchangers to make **1 water -> 10 steam**. Modern ratio is therefore **1 offshore pump : 200 boilers : 400 steam engines** (the classic 1:20:40 is pre-2.0.7). Boiler: 1.8 MW of steam, ~0.45 coal/s (a 4 MJ coal every ~2.2 s), 60 steam/s @ 165°C. Steam engine: 900 kW, 30 steam/s. Guide text updated to the 2.0 ratio.
- Science packs: red (automation) = 1 copper plate + 1 iron gear wheel (5 s, 1 pack); green (logistic) = 1 transport belt + 1 inserter (6 s, 1 pack); military produces 2 packs per cycle; blue (chemical) requires plastic bars -> oil processing. Progression order red -> green -> blue (+ military when needed) -> production -> utility.
- Rocket: Rocket silo research takes all science packs **except military**. 2.0.7 changed the rocket part recipe from rocket control units to processing units: 1 part = 10 low density structure + 10 processing unit + 10 rocket fuel (3 s); 100 parts per rocket = 1,000 of each. A satellite payload returns 1,000 space science packs ~29 s after launch; the first launch triggers the victory screen. (Space Age's much-cheaper rocket is NOT the Wave 0 rule - Waves 0-1 run base-game-compatible with Space Age disabled.)
- Pollution/attacks: machines emit pollution; chunks, trees and spawners absorb it; when a nest's chunk pollution is high, spawners absorb it to muster biters into attacks launched every 1-10 minutes against the source. Evolution rises from time, total pollution produced, and spawner destruction (destroying nests is the big lever). Defense basics: gun turrets (feed magazines), walls, repair packs; do not rely on the starting pistol.
- Keybinds (from the wiki shortcuts page + established 2.0 defaults): pipette Q, rotate R / Shift+R, copy Shift+RMB, paste Shift+LMB, alt-mode Alt, blueprint library B, build ghost Shift+LMB, fast transfer Ctrl+LMB, fast split Ctrl+RMB, filter MMB, camera rotate Shift+wheel. Wiki page does not list movement/game keys; those use standard defaults (WASD, E inventory, M map, P production, N logistics network, ` quickbar pages, 1-0 slots).
