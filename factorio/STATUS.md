## Notes

**Wave 0 fact-check log (Task 4 Step 8, 2026-08-03, sources: wiki.factorio.com):**
- Steam power: 2.0.7 changed boilers/heat exchangers to make **1 water -> 10 steam**. Modern ratio is therefore **1 offshore pump : 200 boilers : 400 steam engines** (the classic 1:20:40 is pre-2.0.7). Boiler: 1.8 MW of steam, ~0.45 coal/s (a 4 MJ coal every ~2.2 s), 60 steam/s @ 165°C. Steam engine: 900 kW, 30 steam/s. Guide text updated to the 2.0 ratio.
- Science packs: red (automation) = 1 copper plate + 1 iron gear wheel (5 s, 1 pack); green (logistic) = 1 transport belt + 1 inserter (6 s, 1 pack); military produces 2 packs per cycle; blue (chemical) requires plastic bars -> oil processing. Progression order red -> green -> blue (+ military when needed) -> production -> utility.
- Rocket: Rocket silo research takes all science packs **except military**. 2.0.7 changed the rocket part recipe from rocket control units to processing units: 1 part = 10 low density structure + 10 processing unit + 10 rocket fuel (3 s); 100 parts per rocket = 1,000 of each. A satellite payload returns 1,000 space science packs ~29 s after launch; the first launch triggers the victory screen. (Space Age's much-cheaper rocket is NOT the Wave 0 rule - Waves 0-1 run base-game-compatible with Space Age disabled.)
- Pollution/attacks: machines emit pollution; chunks, trees and spawners absorb it; when a nest's chunk pollution is high, spawners absorb it to muster biters into attacks launched every 1-10 minutes against the source. Evolution rises from time, total pollution produced, and spawner destruction (destroying nests is the big lever). Defense basics: gun turrets (feed magazines), walls, repair packs; do not rely on the starting pistol.
- Keybinds (from the wiki shortcuts page + established 2.0 defaults): pipette Q, rotate R / Shift+R, copy Shift+RMB, paste Shift+LMB, alt-mode Alt, blueprint library B, build ghost Shift+LMB, fast transfer Ctrl+LMB, fast split Ctrl+RMB, filter MMB, camera rotate Shift+wheel. Wiki page does not list movement/game keys; those use standard defaults (WASD, E inventory, M map, P production, N logistics network, ` quickbar pages, 1-0 slots).

**Wave 1 fact-check log (Task 5 Step 8, 2026-08-03, sources: wiki.factorio.com):**
- Rail: a rail signal divides track into **blocks**; a train only enters a block whose entry signal is green, and all the block's entry signals turn red while it is inside. Chain signals copy the state of the **next** signal along the train's path and guard crossings/interchanges; special case: a chain signal ignores exit signals when its destination is reachable without crossing another signal. The chapter uses the block/signal model.
- Circuit network: red and green wires form **separate** networks (same-colour wires link and sum; a receiver connected to both colours sums them); the constant combinator broadcasts up to 20 values; arithmetic and decider combinators have separate input/output networks; combinators unlock with the circuit-network research. All inserters have had a filter option since 2.0.7.
- Robots: roboport logistic area **50x50**, construction area **110x110**; logistic robot base speed 0.05 tiles/tick (**3 tiles/s**), carries **1 item** by default (up to **4** via worker robot cargo size research); pickup priority active provider > storage/buffer > passive provider; 2.0.7 gave roboports 2-chunk radar and cut robot recharge to 500 kW.
- Modules (2.0 numbers): speed modules 1/2/3 = **+20/+30/+50%** crafting speed with +50/+60/+70% power; efficiency modules = **-30/-40/-50%** energy; productivity modules = **+4/+6/+10%** (reducing speed, raising power); effects are **additive** with energy capped at **-80%**; pollution tracks energy consumption; modules are research-gated and expensive to produce. Beacon: transmits module effects to module-compatible machines in a **9x9** area with **diminishing returns** as more beacons hit one machine (distribution efficiency 1.5 for normal quality, divided by the square root of the beacon count) - the chapter uses the modern 2.0 model, not the old flat 50% model.
- Quality: five tiers (normal/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary); quality modules add **+1/+2/+2.5%** chance per tier; the **recycler returns 25%** of its input (rolling quality again) - the upcycling gate; higher quality adds roughly **+30% per tier** to machine crafting speed and module effect.
- Belts/machines: yellow **15** / red **30** / blue **45** items per second (two lanes per belt, 8 items/tile density); underground belts reach 4/6/8 tiles; assembling machines craft at **0.5/0.75/1.25**. **Bulk inserters** are the base-game high-capacity inserter; **stack inserters are Space Age** (excluded from Waves 0-1). Guide text updated accordingly.
