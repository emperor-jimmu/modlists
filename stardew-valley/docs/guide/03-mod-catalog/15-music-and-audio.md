# Music and Audio

Mods that add or replace music tracks, ambient sounds, and audio effects.

| Mod | Purpose | Notes | Load Order |
|-----|---------|-------|------------|
| Now You Hear It | Adds positional sounds to activities and objects that you'd expect to make noise — footsteps on different surfaces, tools interacting with materials, environmental rustling, and more. All sounds play from in-world positions for a more immersive audio experience. | [NexusMods](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/41626) — CP-based, v1.2.2, 349+ endorsements. Requires Content Patcher and Positional Audio. SpaceCore optional for additional sound effects. | After foundations (after Positional Audio) |

## Notes

- **Now You Hear It** depends on the **Positional Audio** framework (listed in Foundations). Install that first.
- SpaceCore is listed as optional but recommended by the mod author for additional sound coverage.
- This mod is purely auditory — it adds no gameplay mechanics, items, or UI changes.
