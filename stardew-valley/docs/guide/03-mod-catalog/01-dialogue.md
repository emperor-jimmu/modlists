## Notes

- Compatible with Gender Neutrality Mod Tokens, Diverse Stardew Valley, Haywrite's Dialogue Expansion, and More Specific Gift Reactions — all auto-detected.
- Year 1-2 festival dialogue matches vanilla; years 3+ have new sets; year 5+ randomises between all sets.
- Requires SMAPI, Content Patcher, and optionally GMCM for in-game configuration.
- **Sophia SVE Dialogue Expansion** and **Lance Dialogue Expansion Pack** require Stardew Valley Expanded (SVE) — install SVE first. These are optional add-ons for SVE users and do not conflict with broader dialogue mods like CFDE.
- **Haley Ever After - Sweet Edition** is the SFW version of the original Haley Ever After mod. For best results with other dialogue mods (CFDE, True Love Valley), load Haley Ever After last in your Content Patcher load order so its marriage dialogue takes priority.
- **Spouse-specific dialogue mods** (Haley Ever After, Sophia SVE, Lance Expansion, More Romantic Harvey Marriage Dialogue) only affect post-marriage dialogue for their respective NPCs. They do not affect pre-marriage interactions and can be added or removed mid-save without issues.
- **More Romantic Harvey Marriage Dialogue** is a lightweight drop-in mod — no configuration needed. It is compatible with Canon-Friendly Dialogue Expansion, Harvey Marriage Expansion, and other Harvey dialogue mods. Load it after other dialogue mods for priority.
