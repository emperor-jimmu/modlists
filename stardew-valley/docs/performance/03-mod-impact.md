# Mod Performance Impact Results

## Notable Impacts

| Mod | Impact | Details |
|-----|--------|---------|
| SH's Wild Animals | Medium | Adds many critter sprites that spawn based on time/weather/season. Can cause FPS drops on lower-end systems or in areas with dense spawns. Reduce spawn rates in config.json if needed. Not yet benchmarked. |
| Lilybrook | Medium-High | Major world expansion with 17 NPCs, new locations, crops, fish, and special orders. Likely similar impact to East Scarp — expect longer load times and higher memory usage. Not yet benchmarked. |
