# Wave 1: The Horizon Seekers

> **Difficulty: Beginner** — Learn the game under the safest possible conditions with a powerful patron watching your back.

> *"Your species was discovered by an ancient, god-like civilization known as The Keepers. They uplifted you from your homeworld, guided your early development, and now watch over your fledgling empire from their ring world. To the galaxy, you are the favored children of the immortals — protected, privileged, and perhaps a little naive. Your patrons task you with mysterious missions and demand tribute, but their protection ensures no one dares attack you. One day, you may learn the truth behind their ancient purpose — and decide your own destiny."*

## Campaign Goal

**"The Science Victory"** — Explore the galaxy, make friends, out-research everyone, and survive until the end-game crisis. Don't worry about formal victory conditions. Your goal is to reach the end-game with a stable, prosperous empire.

## What You'll Learn

By the end of this wave, you should understand:

- **Economy basics** — How districts, buildings, jobs, and pops create resources. How to read the resource bar and fix deficits.
- **Exploration** — Surveying, anomalies, dig sites, first contact. Why science ships are the most important ships.
- **Planets** — How to specialize planets, manage pops, and avoid unemployment.
- **Diplomacy** — Envoys, improving relations, non-aggression pacts, research agreements, embassies.
- **Basic research** — How the tech tree works, picking relevant techs, managing researchers.
- **Your patron** — How the Scion origin works, tribute demands, patron missions, the eventual succession war.

## Walkthrough Milestones

### Years 1–10: First Steps

1. **Pause immediately.** Queue 2 city districts, 1 industrial district, 1 research lab.
2. Build 2 more science ships (total 3). Survey in different directions.
3. Build a construction ship. Claim adjacent systems.
4. Queue 5 more corvettes. You're protected by your patron, but a fleet helps.
5. When you find 80%+ habitable planets, queue colony ships.

### Years 10–30: Expansion Under Protection

1. Focus on chokepoints. Your patron protects you from invasion, so expand greedily.
2. Your first colony should be self-sufficient by year 15. Start a second by year 20.
3. Keep surveying. Anomalies are your best source of early rewards.
4. Pick Discovery tradition first (faster research + anomaly discovery).
5. **Your patron will demand tribute.** Pay it. The protection is worth it.

### Years 30–100: The Middle Game

1. You should have 3–5 colonies. Specialize each one.
2. Join the Galactic Community when it forms. Vote on resolutions.
3. Build a federation with friendly neighbors.
4. Your patron will eventually give you a **succession war** mission. This is your chance to become independent. Prepare a fleet.
5. Keep research at max. Your goal is tech superiority.

### Years 100–150: Late Game Preparation

1. You should be independent by now (or still under your patron). Either way, build tall.
2. Start megastructure projects (Science Nexus, Dyson Sphere) if you can.
3. Prepare for the end-game crisis. You need a fleet of 100K+ power.
4. Your patron's protection means you face less threat than normal — use this to tech up.

### Years 150+: End-Game

1. The crisis will arrive around 2400. Meet it with your fleet.
2. Even if you lose, you learned. That's the point of Wave 1.

## Graduation Criteria

You're ready for [Wave 2](wave-2-intermediate.md) when you can:

- Reach year 2280 with a **stable positive economy** in all basic resources
- Understand **planet specialization** (not mixing research and industry on the same planet)
- Successfully manage **first contact** and maintain at least one diplomatic agreement
- Build and maintain a **fleet** at or near your naval cap
- Complete the **Scion origin chain** (become independent or choose to serve)
- **Never used the emergency resources** (market, favors) to fix a spiraling deficit

??? tip "Need more practice?"
    Play the wave again with a different Scion build. Try a spiritualist variant or a different authority type. The more comfortable you are with the basics, the more Wave 2 will teach you.
