# Assetto Maximus

A curated modlist and game guide for **Assetto Corsa (2014) v1.16.4**.

Structured in **waves** — each wave adds more mods, mechanics, and complexity alongside a roleplaying narrative. From vanilla to fully-modded, guided every step of the way.

## Contents

- `guide/` — Full guide and modlist by category
- `template/` — Typst template for PDF generation
- `output/` — Generated PDF
- `conflicts.md` — Tracked mod conflicts
- `mod-ideas.md` — Future mod ideas

## Requirements

- **Assetto Corsa** (Steam) v1.16.4+
- **Content Manager** — [Download](https://assettocorsa.club/content-manager.html)
- **Typst 0.15+** — For PDF generation

## Generating the PDF

Run `./generate-pdf.bat` (Windows) to compile the full guide into `output/Assetto-Maximus.pdf`.

## Driving Curriculum

The modlist follows a progressive driving curriculum inspired by professional coaching methods (Suellio Almeida, Ross Bentley):

| Phase | Title | Wave | Focus |
|-------|-------|------|-------|
| 1 | Foundations | Wave 0 | Consistency, confidence, basic car control |
| 2 | Balance & Speed | Wave 1 | Weight transfer, trail braking, slip angle |
| 3 | Cornering Precision | Wave 1 | Advanced techniques, complex corners |
| 4 | Race Craft & Mastery | Wave 2 | Overtaking, racecraft, mental game |

## Waves at a Glance

| Wave | Title | Theme |
|------|-------|-------|
| 0 | The Novice's Tale | Vanilla+ — learn the ropes (Phase 1) |
| 1 | The Apprentice's Rise | Enhanced — balance, speed, precision (Phases 2 & 3) |
| 2 | The Master's Ascent | Mastery — racecraft & competition (Phase 4) |
