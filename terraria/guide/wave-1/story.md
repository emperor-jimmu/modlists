# Wave 1 — Digging Deeper

You wake up again.

The forest is familiar. The slimes are familiar. But something is wrong. The trees are too tall. The night is too dark. And the Guide — Andrew — he's here, but he doesn't remember you.

*"Oh, hello there! I'm Andrew. Your Guide. Yes, I live in this world. No, I don't know how I got here."*

He says it like he's said it a thousand times. Because he has.

You know the drill this time. Chop wood. Build shelter. Survive. But Andrew's jokes feel forced. His tips are slower, like he's trying to remember them. When you ask him about the Eye of Cthulhu, he pauses too long before answering.

*"The Eye... right. It watches. It always watches. But this time, it feels different. Like it knows you're coming back."*

The world has changed. There are new biomes you don't remember. The jungle is deeper. The dungeon is colder. And the enemies — they're smarter. They wait. They learn.

Andrew tries to help. He really does. But his voice cracks when he talks about the Wall of Flesh. He doesn't finish his sentences. He stares at the horizon like he's seeing something you can't.

*"I don't... I don't remember why I knew you'd come back. I just did. And now I'm here again, and I don't know what's different. But something is. Something is wrong."*

By the time you defeat Moon Lord, Andrew is quiet. He doesn't congratulate you. He just looks at you with eyes that are too old for his face.

*"There's another one, isn't there? Another world. I can feel it. But I can't... I can't go with you. I don't think I'm allowed."*

He smiles, but it doesn't reach his eyes.

*"Good luck out there. You'll need it."*

And then you wake up again.
