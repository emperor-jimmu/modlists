## Class Synergies with Modded Gear

Modded content often introduces cross-class weapons and hybrid builds. Here's how to optimize:

### Melee/Ranged Hybrid

**Strategy:**
- Use melee weapons for close-range burst damage.
- Switch to ranged weapons for kiting and boss phases with heavy movement.
- **Recommended Accessories:** Warrior Emblem + Ranger Emblem (use both if the mod allows dual emblems).
- **Armor:** Turtle Armor (melee defense) with ranged weapon swap.

### Magic/Summoner Hybrid

**Strategy:**
- Use summoner minions for passive damage while casting magic spells.
- **Recommended Accessories:** Summoner Emblem + Sorcerer Emblem.
- **Armor:** Spectre Armor (magic) with summoner whip for minion command.

### Hybrid Build Tips

- **Mana Management:** Hybrid builds consume mana faster. Use Mana Regeneration accessories and potions.
- **Accessory Slots:** You have 5-7 accessory slots. Balance damage, defense, and utility.
- **Reforging:** Prioritize damage prefixes for your main damage type, utility prefixes for secondary.

## Post-Moon Lord Content

Modded content often extends the game beyond Moon Lord. Here's what to expect:

### Typical Post-Moon Lord Progression

1. **Loot Moon Lord:** Craft endgame weapons and armor from his drops.
2. **Explore Modded Biomes:** New biomes often have post-Moon Lord enemies and loot.
3. **Fight Modded Superbosses:** These are harder than Moon Lord and require optimized gear.
4. **Craft Post-Moon Lord Gear:** Use drops from superbosses to craft even stronger equipment.
5. **Repeat:** Some mods have multiple tiers of post-Moon Lord content.

### Optimization Tips

- **Min-max your build:** Reforge all accessories to Menacing (+4% damage each).
- **Use all buffs:** Food, potions, banners, and NPC buffs stack.
- **Arena optimization:** Add every buff station (campfire, heart lantern, bast statue, sunflower, etc.).
- **Weapon swap:** Use different weapons for different boss phases.

## Secret/Legendary Seed Strategies

Terraria has secret seeds that change world generation:

### "For the Worthy" Seed

**Seed:** `for the worthy` (case-insensitive)

**Changes:**
- All enemies have +100% health and +50% damage
- Bosses have unique attack patterns
- World generation is inverted (Hallow and Corruption/Crimson swapped)
- NPCs sell unique items

**Strategy:**
- Play cautiously. Enemies hit much harder.
- Use ranged weapons to kite enemies.
- Build arenas with more healing stations than usual.
- Expect boss fights to take 2-3x longer.

### "Get Fixed Boi" Seed

**Seed:** `get fixed boi` (case-insensitive)

**Changes:**
- World generation is chaotic (multiple biomes mixed together)
- Enemies spawn in unusual places
- Some items have unique effects

**Strategy:**
- Explore carefully. The world layout is unpredictable.
- Build your base in a safe area (forest or snow).
- Use the map frequently to navigate.

## Modded Biome Navigation

Content mods add new biomes. Here's how to navigate them:

### General Tips

- **Read the mod documentation:** Each mod's Workshop page explains its biomes.
- **Bring exploration gear:** Spelunker Potions, Hunter Potions, and a good light source.
- **Prepare for danger:** Modded biomes often have enemies stronger than vanilla Hardmode.
- **Build a base nearby:** Set up a pylon and NPC housing for easy access.

### Common Modded Biome Types

- **New Underground Biomes:** Often have unique ores, enemies, and loot. Treat them like vanilla Hardmode biomes.
- **Surface Biomes:** May have unique enemies and events. Build arenas for farming.
- **Dimension Biomes:** Some mods add alternate dimensions (e.g., The Aether, The Void). Bring full gear before entering.

## Player Challenges

These are self-imposed restriction rulesets. They're not required, but they add replayability and test your mastery.

### Challenge 1: Class Purity

**Rules:**
- Pick one damage type at character creation (melee, ranged, magic, or summoner).
- You may never deal damage with any other type.
- For summoners, whips are allowed (they command minions).
- Tool damage (pickaxe, axe) is allowed for mining/building, not combat.

**Strategy:**
- **Melee:** Focus on defense and healing. Use shields and regen accessories.
- **Ranged:** Stock up on ammo. Use homing bullets for bosses.
- **Magic:** Manage mana carefully. Use mana-regen accessories.
- **Summoner:** Keep minions alive. Use whips to focus boss weak points.

### Challenge 2: No Crafting

**Rules:**
- You may only use items found in chests, dropped by enemies, or purchased from NPCs.
- No crafting stations allowed.
- You cannot craft weapons, armor, potions, or accessories.

**Strategy:**
- **Explore extensively.** Loot every chest. Kill every enemy.
- **Trade with NPCs.** Buy weapons and accessories from the Merchant, Arms Dealer, etc.
- **Farm bosses.** Some bosses drop crafted items directly.
- **Accept limitations.** You won't have endgame gear. Play defensively.

### Challenge 3: Hardcore Adjacent (Mediumcore)

**Rules:**
- Play a Mediumcore character (you drop all items on death).
- No exceptions. If you die, you lose everything.

**Strategy:**
- **Backup gear caches:** Store backup equipment in chests near boss arenas.
- **Teleporter recovery network:** Build teleporters to quickly retrieve dropped items.
- **Overprepare for bosses:** Don't fight a boss until you're 100% ready.
- **Use the Nurse NPC:** Teleport to the Nurse for emergency healing (costs coins).

### Challenge 4: One Life (Hardcore)

**Rules:**
- Play a Hardcore character (permanent death).
- If you die, the character is dead forever.

**Strategy:**
- **Extreme caution:** Avoid unnecessary risks. Don't fight bosses until you're overgeared.
- **Skip dangerous content:** Some bosses (Duke Fishron, Martian Madness) are too risky. Skip them.
- **Focus on defense:** Use the highest-defense armor, shields, and regen accessories.
- **Stock up on healing:** Carry 50+ healing potions for boss fights.
- **Use the Nurse NPC:** Never fight a boss without a Nurse nearby for emergency healing.

## Appendix: Master/Legendary Difficulty

### Master Mode

**Changes from Expert:**
- Enemies have +20% health and +10% damage (on top of Expert bonuses)
- Bosses have unique Master-exclusive drops (relics, pets)
- Some bosses have new attack patterns

**Strategy:**
- Play even more cautiously than Expert.
- Use all available buffs and arena optimizations.
- Expect boss fights to take 20-30% longer than Expert.

### Legendary Difficulty (For the Worthy + Master Mode)

**Changes:**
- Combines For the Worthy seed with Master Mode
- Enemies have massive health and damage
- Bosses are significantly harder

**Strategy:**
- This is the ultimate challenge. Only attempt if you've mastered Master Mode.
- Use every optimization: best gear, full buffs, optimized arenas.
- Expect to die multiple times before succeeding.
