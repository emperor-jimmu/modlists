# Wave 0 — First Steps

**Difficulty:** Classic (Softcore)
**World:** Medium, Corruption recommended
**Seed:** Random

## Introduction

Welcome to Terraria. This guide assumes you've never played before. We'll cover everything from your first steps to defeating the final boss. Take your time — there's no rush.

## Controls & Settings

**Movement:**
- **A / D** or **Left / Right arrows** — Move left/right
- **Space** — Jump (hold to jump higher)
- **S** or **Down arrow** — Crouch / use rope / drop through platforms

**Combat & Interaction:**
- **Left Click** — Use item / attack
- **Right Click** — Use item (secondary action) / interact with objects
- **Shift** — Use item (tertiary action, varies by item)

**Inventory & UI:**
- **Esc** — Open inventory / pause menu
- **E** — Open inventory (alternative)
- **1-9** or **Mouse Wheel** — Select hotbar slot
- **Tab** — Toggle map

**Recommended Settings for Beginners:**
- **Difficulty:** Classic (you keep your items on death)
- **Map:** Enable auto-map (F11 to toggle fullscreen map)
- **Interface Scale:** 100% (default)
- **Lighting:** Color (easiest to see)

## Your First Day

You spawn in a random biome. Your first 3 minutes are critical:

1. **Chop trees.** Hit trees with your copper axe until you have 10-15 wood.
2. **Craft a workbench.** Open inventory (Esc), place wood in the crafting grid, craft a Workbench. Place it on the ground.
3. **Craft basic tools.** At the workbench, craft:
   - Wooden Sword (for defense)
   - Wooden Pickaxe (for mining)
   - Torch (wood + gel from slimes)
4. **Build shelter.** Before nightfall (about 4 minutes in), build a small enclosed room:
   - Walls (wood platforms or wood blocks)
   - A door
   - A light source (torch)
   - A flat surface (workbench counts)
   - A comfort item (chair or table)
5. **Survive the night.** Stay inside. Zombies and Demon Eyes will spawn outside. Fight them through walls or wait for dawn.

**Tip:** If you die, you respawn at your spawn point (default: world spawn). Your items drop where you died — retrieve them quickly.

## NPC Housing

NPCs (Non-Player Characters) move into your world when certain conditions are met. They need valid housing:

**Requirements:**
- Enclosed room (walls on all 4 sides, including background walls)
- Minimum size: 6 blocks wide × 6 blocks tall (interior space)
- Maximum size: 750 blocks total (don't build mansions)
- At least one light source (torch)
- At least one flat surface (table, workbench, etc.)
- At least one comfort item (chair, bed, etc.)
- At least one door
- No corruption/crimson nearby (within ~50 blocks)

**Biome Preferences:**
- **Guide:** No preference (spawns automatically)
- **Merchant:** Needs 50 silver coins in your inventory
- **Nurse:** Needs to heal you at least once
- **Demolitionist:** Needs an explosive in your inventory
- **Arms Dealer:** Needs a gun in your inventory
- **Dryad:** Defeat Eater of Worlds or Brain of Cthulhu

**Tip:** Build housing in clusters near safe biomes (forest, snow). NPCs prefer to live near each other. Use the housing menu (Esc → Housing UI) to assign NPCs to specific rooms.

## Boss Progression

Terraria has 14 main bosses. Here's the recommended order and basic strategy for each:

### Pre-Hardmode

**1. Eye of Cthulhu**
- **Trigger:** Have 200+ health, 3 NPCs, and it spawns randomly at night (or craft Suspicious Looking Eye to summon)
- **Strategy:** Build a long platform (100+ blocks). Use a bow or magic weapon. Dodge its dash attacks. Kill the servants first if they appear.
- **Drops:** Demonite/Crimtane Ore, binoculars (rare)

**2. Eater of Worlds (Corruption) / Brain of Cthulhu (Crimson)**
- **Trigger:** Destroy 3 Shadow Orbs (Corruption) or Crimson Hearts in the evil biome
- **Strategy:** For Eater of Worlds, use piercing weapons (spears, Vilethorn). Kill segments. For Brain, destroy the Creepers first, then focus the Brain.
- **Drops:** Demonite/Crimtane Ore, Tissue Samples, monster masks

**3. Queen Bee**
- **Trigger:** Find a Larva in the Underground Jungle, break it
- **Strategy:** Build an arena with platforms in the jungle cave. Use ranged or magic. Dodge her charge attacks and bee swarms.
- **Drops:** Bee Gun, Bee Wax (for crafting), Honey Comb accessory

**4. Skeletron**
- **Trigger:** Talk to the Old Man at the Dungeon entrance at night
- **Strategy:** Build a long platform. Use ranged weapons. Kill the Hands first (they regenerate the head). Dodge the skull's fire attacks.
- **Drops:** Bone key, Bone Pickaxe, Book of Skulls (magic weapon)

**5. Wall of Flesh**
- **Trigger:** Throw a Guide Voodoo Doll into lava in the Underworld (Hell)
- **Preparation:** Build a long bridge (500+ blocks) across Hell. Stock up on healing potions, ranged weapons, and arrows.
- **Strategy:** Run backward while shooting. Kill the Hungry (the mouth tentacles) first. Focus the eye or mouth weak point. Dodge the laser attacks.
- **Result:** Hardmode begins. The world changes dramatically.

### Hardmode

**6. The Destroyer / The Twins / Skeletron Prime**
- **Trigger:** Smash 3 Demon/Crimson Altars in the world (after Wall of Flesh)
- **Strategy:**
  - **Destroyer:** Use piercing weapons (Daedalus Stormbow, Nimbus Rod). Build a box arena with a hole at the bottom.
  - **Twins:** Focus Retinazer (second phase) first. Use ranged or magic. Dodge laser attacks.
  - **Skeletron Prime:** Kill the Prime Cannon and Prime Laser first. Use ranged weapons. Dodge the spinning head.
- **Drops:** Souls of Might, Souls of Sight, Souls of Fright, Hallowed Bars

**7. Plantera**
- **Trigger:** Defeat all 3 mechanical bosses, then break a Plantera's Bulb in the Underground Jungle
- **Strategy:** Build a large arena in the jungle (200×200 blocks). Use ranged weapons with Chlorophyte bullets. Dodge her tentacles and spore clouds. Kill the tentacles first to reduce her attacks.
- **Drops:** Temple Key, Jungle trophy items, coins

**8. Golem**
- **Trigger:** Defeat Plantera, then break 4 Lihzahrd Altars in the Jungle Temple
- **Strategy:** Build a platform arena. Use ranged or magic. Kill the hands first (they power up the head). Dodge the fire fists and laser beams.
- **Drops:** Shiny Red Balloon, Golem trophy items, Temple keys

**9. Duke Fishron**
- **Trigger:** Fish in the Ocean with a Truffle Worm as bait
- **Strategy:** Build a long bridge over the ocean. Use ranged weapons with Holy Arrows. Dodge his bubble attacks and tornado spins. Phase 2 and 3 are much harder — consider skipping until post-Moon Lord.
- **Drops:** Flairon (summon weapon), Bubble Gun, Storm Spear

**10. Lunatic Cultist**
- **Trigger:** Kill the 4 Cultists at the Dungeon entrance (after defeating Golem)
- **Strategy:** Use ranged or magic. Dodge his shadow clones and lightning attacks. Don't attack the clones — only the real Cultist takes damage.
- **Drops:** None (triggers the Lunar Events)

**11. Moon Lord**
- **Trigger:** Defeat the 4 Celestial Pillars during the Lunar Events
- **Preparation:** Craft Solar Flare armor (melee), Vortex armor (ranged), Nebula armor (magic), or Stardust armor (summoner). Craft endgame weapons from pillar fragments.
- **Strategy:** Kill the hands first (they heal the head). Focus the head. Dodge the Phantasmal Deathbolt. Use healing potions and heart lanterns.
- **Drops:** Meowmere, Terrarian, Last Prism, Celebration MK2, and other endgame weapons

## Class Basics

Terraria has 4 main damage types. Each has strengths and weaknesses:

**Melee:**
- **Strengths:** High damage, high defense, good survivability
- **Weaknesses:** Short range, slow movement
- **Weapons:** Swords, spears, flails, yoyos
- **Recommended for:** Players who like getting up close

**Ranged:**
- **Strengths:** Good damage at range, versatile ammo types
- **Weaknesses:** Ammo-dependent, moderate defense
- **Weapons:** Bows, guns, launchers
- **Recommended for:** Players who prefer keeping distance

**Magic:**
- **Strengths:** High burst damage, utility spells
- **Weaknesses:** Mana-dependent, low defense
- **Weapons:** Staffs, tomes, guns that use mana
- **Recommended for:** Players who like spellcasting and variety

**Summoner:**
- **Strengths:** Passive damage (minions attack automatically), good for multitasking
- **Weaknesses:** Low personal damage, minions can die
- **Weapons:** Whips (to command minions), summon staves
- **Recommended for:** Players who like a hands-off approach

**Tip:** You can mix classes, but focusing on one makes progression easier. Switch classes mid-game if you want a change.

## Biome Exploration Order

Explore biomes in this order for the safest progression:

1. **Surface Forest** — Starting biome. Safe during the day.
2. **Underground** — Below the surface. Mine ore, find chests, encounter basic enemies.
3. **Snow Biome** — Cold biome. Ice enemies, Ice Mimics. Good for early armor.
4. **Desert** — Sand biome. Antlions, vultures. Find Ancient Battle Armor.
5. **Jungle** — Dangerous. Poisonous plants, jungle enemies. Find the Jungle Temple.
6. **Dungeon** — Very dangerous. Cursed Skulls, Dungeon Guardians. Need to defeat Skeletron first for safe entry.
7. **Underworld (Hell)** — Lava and fire enemies. Need to defeat Wall of Flesh to enter Hardmode.
8. **Hardmode Surface** — New enemies spawn. Hallow and Corruption/Crimson spread.
9. **Underground Hardmode** — New ores, harder enemies.
10. **Jungle Temple** — Post-Plantera. Need Temple Key from Plantera.
11. **Dungeon Hardmode** — Post-Plantera. New enemies and loot.

## Fishing

Fishing is a major mechanic for crafting materials, quest fish, and rare items:

**Basics:**
- Craft a fishing rod (Wooden Pole is free, upgrade to Fiberglass or Mechanic's Rod)
- Craft bait (worms, bugs, or buy from the Merchant)
- Find a body of water (minimum 300 tiles for best results)
- Cast (left click), wait for the bobber to dip, then reel in (left click again)

**Tips:**
- Fishing power = Rod power + Bait power + Accessories + Potions
- Use Sonar Potions to see what you're catching before reeling
- Use Crate Potions to catch crates (contain random loot)
- Quest fish are needed for the Angler NPC's daily quests

## Potion Crafting

Potions are essential for boss fights and exploration:

**Basic Potions (craft at Alchemy Table or Bottles):**
- **Healing Potion** — Restores health (craft with Mushroom + Gel)
- **Mana Potion** — Restores mana (craft with Star + Gel)
- **Ironskin Potion** — +8 defense (craft with Iron Ore + Mushroom)
- **Regeneration Potion** — Health regen over time (craft with Mushroom + Daybloom)
- **Swiftness Potion** — +25% movement speed (craft with Cactus + Daybloom)
- **Hunter Potion** — See enemies on the map (craft with Shark Fin + Daybloom)

**Tips:**
- Stock up before boss fights (20-30 healing potions minimum)
- Use an Alchemy Table to save materials (33% chance to not consume ingredients)
- Grow herbs in Clay Pots for infinite supply

## Hardmode Transition

Defeating the Wall of Flesh triggers Hardmode. The world changes dramatically:

**Immediate Changes:**
- A V-shaped stripe of Hallow and Corruption/Crimson cuts across the world
- New enemies spawn everywhere (much stronger)
- New ores appear (Cobalt, Mythril, Adamantite)
- New biomes form (Hallow, Underground Hallow, Underground Corruption/Crimson)

**What to Do:**
1. **Don't panic.** Return to your base and fortify it.
2. **Mine the new ores.** You need Cobalt/Palladium to craft Hardmode armor.
3. **Upgrade your gear.** Hardmode enemies hit hard — you need better equipment.
4. **Fight the mechanical bosses.** Smash 3 altars to trigger them.
5. **Manage biome spread.** Use the Clentaminator to purify or spread biomes.

**Tip:** Hardmode is a second game. The progression loop is the same: explore, mine, craft, fight bosses. The enemies are just stronger and the stakes are higher.
