# Wave 0 — First Steps

*Oh, hello there!*

I'm Andrew. Your Guide. Yes, I live in this world. No, I don't know how I got here. All I know is that you just showed up, and now we're stuck together. Lucky me.

Let's start with the obvious: you don't know what you're doing. That's fine. Everyone starts somewhere. Mine happens to be standing next to a clueless adventurer in a forest full of slimes.

Here's the deal: during the day, you gather wood, mine ore, build a house. At night, you hide. Zombies will show up. Floating eyes will try to eat your face. It's not personal — it's just Tuesday.

I'll tell you what you need to know. When to build a house. How to craft a sword. Which way is down (spoiler: it's always down). I'll even warn you about the Eye of Cthulhu, though honestly, the first time it shows up is always a surprise.

You'll get better. I've seen it before. The copper shortsword becomes a gold broadsword. The wooden bow becomes a phoenix blaster. You'll learn that the jungle is terrifying, the dungeon is worse, and hell is... well, you'll see.

And when you finally beat Moon Lord — that giant floating eyeball thing at the end of everything — you'll think you're done. You'll think you've seen it all.

But I'll be here. Waiting. Because there are other worlds. Other layers. And they're waiting for you too.

For now, though? Just survive the night. I'll handle the rest.

*— Andrew*
