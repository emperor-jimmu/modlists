# Getting Started

This modpack is designed around four pillars: **serious power fantasy**, an **easy-to-standard difficulty curve**, **200h+ content runway**, and **chill living** at every stage. You can always stop optimizing and just build something pretty.

---

## First Session

1. **Spawn, punch trees, make a crafting table.** Standard Minecraft start.
2. **Build a starter shelter.** Place a few Sophisticated Storage barrels for early organization (shift-right-click to rename them).
3. **Craft a Waystone** (3 stone + 1 ender pearl) and place it near your base — you can teleport between waystones with no cost.
4. **Find a village** and recruit Guard Villagers (right-click a villager with a sword or buy from the Guard Villager structure). They'll keep creepers and zombies off your lawn.
5. **Place your first MineColonies Building Hut** (the Supply Camp or Supply Ship from the starter kit). The colony is optional early on — it's a long-term project.
6. **Start basic Ars Nouveau.** Craft a Worn Notebook and follow the beginner spells (Break, Place Block, Heal). Even apprentice-tier magic makes day-to-day life smoother.
7. **Cook real food.** Farmer's Delight adds cuttings, cooking pots, and hearty meals — far better than vanilla hunger bars.
8. **Explore.** Waystones spawn in villages — right-click to claim them for free teleports. YUNG's Better Dungeons and Mineshafts make exploration rewarding.

> **Don't rush.** Phase 1 (hours 0–40) is intentionally forgiving. Build a home, plant a garden, learn the mods one at a time. If you die, Corail Tombstone keeps your XP and protects your grave for 24h.

## Progression Overview

### Phase 1 — Cozy Roots (hours 0–40)
Punch trees → starter home → Sophisticated Storage → Guard Villagers → first MineColonies hut → early Mekanism (metallurgic infuser, enrichment chamber) → apprentice Ars Nouveau → Farmer's Delight kitchen → Waystones for fast travel.

Focus: shelter, food, basic automation. The world is safe. Everything is optional.

### Phase 2 — Industrial Ascent (hours 40–200)
Mekanism mid/late-tier (digital miner, ore quintupling, fission reactor) → AE2 ME storage system → Create factories and contraptions → MineColonies city → mid-tier Ars Nouveau spellbook → Create: Big Cannons for colony defense.

Focus: deep automation, logistics puzzles, resource multiplication. Every session unlocks something new.

### Phase 3 — Perpetual Ascent (hours 200+)
Mekanism endgame (fusion reactor, antimatter, Mekasuit) → AE2 quantum networks → Create mega-factories → god-tier Ars Nouveau spells → MineColonies empire across biomes → The Aether, Twilight Forest, L_Ender's Cataclysm for combat endgame.

Focus: endless optimization, personal power ceiling, megabuilds. Also: still perfectly valid to take a break and decorate your house.

> No mechanic forces you into the next phase. If you want to spend 40 hours building a pretty village, you can. The difficulty ramps at YOUR pace.

## Chill Living Rules

- **Death is forgiving.** Corail Tombstone keeps your XP and protects your grave for 24 real-time hours.
- **Waystones are free.** No XP or item cost for teleporting (config `enableCosts = false`). Press the inventory button to open the teleport menu.
- **Creepers don't destroy blocks.** The No Creeper Grief data pack prevents block damage while keeping interactive mobs active.
- **Raid frequency is reduced.** MineColonies raids happen every 12 days instead of 7.
- **You can always stop.** Gardening, building, decorating — all valid game states at any power level.

## Finding Your Way

Key mods you'll encounter early and how to interact with them:

**Sophisticated Storage / Backpacks**
Shift-right-click a barrel or backpack to rename it. Backpacks open with `B` (configurable). Both can be upgraded with iron, gold, diamond tiers.

**JEI (Recipe Browser)**
Press `R` on any item to see how to craft it. Press `U` to see what it's used in. Hover over a fuel item in a furnace recipe to see burn time. This is your primary learning tool for the entire pack.

**The "Ponder" system**
Press `W` on any Create block or item (or press `W` while hovering in JEI) to open an animated tutorial that walks through exactly how the component works.

**JourneyMap**
Press `J` to open the full-screen map. Waypoints, death markers, and automatic mapping of every chunk you visit. Left-click on the map to create a waypoint.

## In-Game Learning Tools

| Mod           | How to Learn                                                                                                                                 |
|---------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **JEI**       | Press `R` on any item to see recipes, `U` to see uses. The recipe browser is your primary tool for learning every mod in the pack.          |
| **Create**    | Press `W` on any Create block or item to open the **Ponder** system — an interactive, animated tutorial that explains each component step by step. |
| **Ars Nouveau** | Craft the **Worn Notebook** — it's the complete in-game guide covering all spells, glyphs, and mechanics.                                |
| **Mekanism**  | Craft the **Tablet** — the in-game guide for all machines, tiers, and progression.                                                          |
| **MineColonies** | The **Build Tool** includes a handbook. Also open the in-game **Help** menu for colony management guides.                               |
