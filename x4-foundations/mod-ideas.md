# Mod Ideas

Ideas for future mods to develop for the Stellar Dominion modlist. These are NOT included in the current release.

## Total Conversions & Galaxy Expansions

- **Star Wars Interworlds** — the flagship X4 total conversion. Conflicts with VRO and most of this list, so it needs its own MO2 profile; would be a separate "Star Wars" wave path.
- **XRSGE (Realspace Galaxy Expansion)** — massively expands the galaxy map. Currently left out because it changes sector layouts that VRO and FOCW expect; revisit with a dedicated profile.

## Ship Packs

- **Ship Variation Expansion (SVE)** — adds faction ship variants. Author retired; the community 9.0 port and VRO patch are workshop-only right now. Revisit once a stable Nexus release exists.
- **XR ShipPack** — X Rebirth ships. Older, stability-problematic, and needs a VRO-trimmed version; only worth adding if the trimmed variant returns.

## Economy & Logistics

- **DeadAir Eco (adopted by Chem O'Dun)** — economy overhaul, natural companion to Reemergence/FOCW. Consider when pairing the wave-3 war mods with a full economy redesign.
- **Faster Crew Leveling** — currently redundant with Learning All The Things; include only if the default LATT pace feels too slow.

## Gameplay Depth

- **Emergent Missions (kuertee)** — dynamic escort/patrol/search-and-destroy missions driven by live events.
- **Head Hunter** — accumulating bounties and bounty hunters. Currently feels OP at default settings; include with reduced bounty values.
- **Roaming Fleet** — lets a fleet commander patrol a sector, kill hostiles, loot and return.
- **Go Fix Your Face** — one-click repair order for damaged ships.
- **Fighter Repairs** — carriers with repair drones slowly patch up docked S/M ships.

## Immersion & Polish

- **More Ship Rooms** — walkable interiors on capital ships.
- **More NPC** — busier station interiors.
- **Sane Office Ambience** — removes the annoying ambient sounds in the PHQ office.
- **Crystal Rarities** — color-coded crystal glow; currently considered cheat-y, so excluded — add only as an explicit difficulty-lowering option.
