The workshop's top-subscribed (top ~90) and top-rated (top 30) lists are dominated by QoL/UI/tools; the single largest content transformation is the BORDERLINE candidate below.
- BORDERLINE candidate (controller decision required): **Ronivan's Legacy - Industrial Revolution** (https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3557584850) — by Sgt_Imalas (preserving Ronivan's posthumous work). 41,158 subscribers, 1,239 ratings, 108,641 unique visitors; #2 top-rated ONI workshop mod. Consolidates 12 industrial mods (Chemical Processing - Biochemistry & Industrial Overhaul, Custom Generators, Custom Reservoirs, Dupes Engineering/Logistics/Machinery/Refrigeration, High Pressure Applications, Mineral Processing - Metallurgy & Mining, Nuclear Processing) into one configurable modpack with per-building toggles. Adds ~37 new elements, ~32+ new buildings, new production chains (biochem, metallurgy, mining, nuclear). Page frames it as "a massive expansion to regular progression... some mods alone could even be considered a DLC in scope." VERDICT: strong near-miss — massive content-expansion of the industrial pillar (K2-scale IN CONTENT, NOT a restructure of base progression/tech tree). If the controller treats "content transformation of industrial progression" as sufficient, Wave 2 could anchor on it (needs its own §6.4 verification + power-spike gate later); otherwise the curated capstone set stands.
