# Primordial Outpost Reborn - Oxygen Not Included Modlist & Guide

A game guide and modlist scaffold for **Oxygen Not Included** (Build 744825, released 2026-07-28; DLCs: Spaced Out!, Bionic Booster Pack), organized in three progressively harder "waves" (0-2), each with its own roleplay backstory.

- Modlist: **27 verified mods** across the three waves (5 mechanics + 7 graphics in Wave 0; 2 mechanics + 4 content + 4 graphics in Wave 1; 2 mechanics + 2 content + 1 graphics in Wave 2) — see STATUS.md for the decision log
- Mod organizer: Mod Updater (verified; see STATUS.md)
- Mod source: Steam Workshop (app 457140)
- PDF: run `build.bat` to regenerate `output/primordial-outpost-reborn.pdf` (33 pages)

## Mods per wave

| Wave | Theme | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 0 - The First Breathe | UI/QoL essentials (vanilla base) | 12 mods (5 mechanics, 7 graphics) |
| 1 - The Vent Tamer | Content + mechanics (no power spikes) | 10 mods (2 mechanics, 4 content, 4 graphics) |
| 2 - The Voyager | Spaced Out capstone | 5 mods (2 mechanics, 2 content, 1 graphics) |
