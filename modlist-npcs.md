# NPCs

**MO2 Separator:** `NPCs` → `NPCs - Appearance`, `NPCs - Population`, `NPCs - Followers`

All mods in this section belong to one of the three NPC separators as noted per subsection.

> Creature/enemy content moved to → [Creatures and Enemies](modlist-creatures.md).

---

## NPC Appearance Overhauls → separator: `NPCs - Appearance`

Face, hair, and presentation direction for Skyrim's named NPCs. Does not re-decide the body/skin/baseline character-visual stack already owned by → `Characters & Creatures`.

### Baseline
- **Northbourne NPCs of Whiterun Hold** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/35404)) — Modern, regionally textured look. Grounded route.
- **The Men of Winter SSE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/10902)) — Dedicated male coverage. Last updated August 2020 — verify 1.6.1170 compatibility.
- **AI Overhaul SSE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/21654)) — NPC schedule and behavior layer. Pairs with any appearance route.
- **Children of the First - An Altmer NPC Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/122167)) — Race-specific modular overlay for Altmer.
- **Children of the Ash - A Dunmer NPC Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/122165)) — Race-specific modular overlay for Dunmer.

### Alternatives
- **High Poly True to Vanilla NPC Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/74226)) — Vanilla-faithful fallback.
- **Pandorable's NPCs (AIO)** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/78524)) — Beauty-forward branch.
- **DIbella's Blessing** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/82606)) — 233 female characters.
- **Males Of Skyrim by zzjay** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/37485))
- **The Wolven Widow - SerketHetyt's Elisif Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/62209))
- **Pride of Skyrim** — Later modular target.

## Civilian And Traveler Population Additions → separator: `NPCs - Population`

Extra background people making towns, inns, and roads feel used — without re-deciding city feel and roadside atmosphere.

### Baseline
- **Crowded Streets** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/127723)) — Compatibility-first crowd: zero cell edits, dynamic cleanup, configurable density.

### Alternatives
- **Travellers of Skyrim** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/1973)) — Road-traveler route. LE-era port — verify 1.6.1170 compatibility.
- **Populated Skyrim Reborn SSE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32190)) — Heavier legacy route.
- **MINPCs** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/29483)) — Adventurer-style incidental content.
- **Immersive Citizens - AI Overhaul SE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/173)) — Known conflict with AI Overhaul SSE.
- **Interesting NPCs SE (3DNPC)** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/29194)) — Authored content NPCs.

## Custom Followers → separator: `NPCs - Followers`

Custom fully-voiced follower additions with authored dialogue, quest commentary, and personal content.

### Baseline
- **Lucien - Immersive Fully Voiced Male Follower** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/20035)) — 5000+ lines, personal quest, LoTD-aware. 30K+ endorsements.
- **Remiel - Custom Voiced Dwemer Specialist** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/51874)) — ~5000 lines. Custom framework — do not use with NFF/AFT.
- **INIGO** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/1461)) — 7000+ lines, 100K+ endorsements.
    - **Inigo Official Patch SE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/62868)) — Required for 1.6.1170.
    - **Lulu's INIGO 2.0** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/126199)) — High-poly visual replacer.
- **Khajiit Will Follow** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/2227)) — 4 Khajiit followers. ~8K endorsements. Compatible with NFF.
- **Song of the Green (Auri Follower)** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/11278)) — 1000+ lines, romance option, Inigo banter.
    - **Majestic Auri** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/72361)) — High-poly visual replacer. 224K+ downloads.
- **Sofia - The Funny Fully Voiced Follower** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/2180)) — Comic relief. May be cut for tonal mismatch.
- **Kaidan 2** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/19075)) — 1200+ lines, romance arc. Use the Immersive Features AIO from kaidanmod.com (supersedes deprecated Nexus Extended Edition).
    - **A Makeover for Kaidan** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/100586))
    - **Immersive Kaidan Start** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/64064))

### Follower Framework Notes
- Remiel and Auri work best outside NFF (custom systems).
- Sofia works under NFF or standalone.
- Lucien tested with NFF but works standalone.
- Kaidan 2 has custom AI system — use built-in track/summon powers instead of NFF.

### Notes
- Remiel WILL BREAK under NFF/AFT. Same for Auri 2.0+.
- Kaidan 2: use kaidanmod.com AIO path (Nexus Extended Edition is deprecated).
- Sofia's comedic tone may clash with grim-dark presentation.
- Verify cross-follower banter (Remiel+Auri have Inigo patches; Kaidan 2 has extended banter with Remiel).
- `Backported Extended ESL Support` listed for Majestic Auri does not apply to 1.6.1170 — do not install.
