## Content

- OvN Lost World (id 3291116635) — settlement-skin / campaign-map visual flavour
  pack from the OvN team; currently considered graphics, not yet selected.
- Walk with the Dragon English-Patch submod — the Cathay mega-pack is
  Chinese-by-default; an English patch submod exists if localization is needed.
- Landmarks of Eternity — IEE Edition submod (id 3079915520) — official pairing
  of landmarks with Immortal Empires Expanded; currently referenced, could be
  listed as an explicit entry.
- Additional Cataph content — his other WH3 releases follow the same 8.1
  "It Works" update pattern and could extend the TEB family.

## Mechanics

- Radious Total War Mod (id 2791750313, Parts 1–4) — full alternative to SFO:
  549 custom units, AI and diplomacy rework. Mutually exclusive with SFO;
  only viable as a full wave-2 swap, never alongside.
- Cost-based Army Caps [EoM] (id 2843367951) — economy-driven anti-doomstack
  alternative to Tabletop Caps; flagged end-of-maintenance, not selected.
- Campaign AI Tweaks (id 3485519396) — alternative campaign-AI push; excluded
  because its author forbids combining with AI Recruitment & Army Compositions.
- Victory Conditions Overhaul routes for newly added factions — VCO updates
  faction routes as DLC/content mods arrive; re-check after each content wave.

## UI / QoL

- The Tooltip Project currently conflicts with SFO per its author; if the
  author adds an SFO-compatible mode, it can move back into Wave 2+.
- Community Bug Fix Mod (CBFM) — distributed off-Workshop (GitHub/Discord) after
  removal; could be documented as an optional manual install if the team
  resumes updates for 8.1.x.
- Multiplayer compatibility work: MIXER currently single-player-only after the
  Aug 2026 CA update; revisit when MP is restored.
