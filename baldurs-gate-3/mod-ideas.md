## Notes

- Nexus Mods search is JS-rendered and heavily biased toward showing translation packs first. Finding the English base mod for popular mods requires visiting translation pages and extracting requirements links, or searching by author name.
- Many mods listed above definitely exist on Nexus but their exact mod IDs couldn't be determined in the time available for initial curation.
- All mods in the verified Wave 1 list have been individually confirmed via Playwright browser page visits.
