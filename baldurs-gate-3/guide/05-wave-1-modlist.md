# Wave 1 Modlist

> **Verification status:** All URLs on this page have been individually verified via Playwright browser. Mods whose URLs could not be confirmed are listed in `mod-ideas.md`.

## Graphics & Visuals

### [Party Limit Begone](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/327)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Removes the 4-player party limit, allowing you to bring all companions at once. Experience every companion's banter, reactions, and story beats in a single playthrough. The most popular gameplay mod for BG3.
- **Dependencies:** Script Extender
- **Category:** Gameplay Mechanics
- **Notes:** Combine with Tactician Plus or Combat Extender to balance the increased party size with harder enemies. Start a new game after installing.

### [Aether's No Party Limits](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/3479)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** An alternative party limit remover with additional configuration options. Allows setting a custom maximum party size, controlling which companions can join, and managing party composition limits.
- **Dependencies:** Script Extender
- **Category:** Gameplay Mechanics
- **Notes:** Use this OR Party Limit Begone — not both.

### [Better Inventory UI](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/4597)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Overhauls the inventory interface with larger grids, better item categorization, clearer icons, and improved tooltip placement. Essential when managing 6-8 characters instead of 4.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** UI/QoL
- **Notes:** Dramatically improves inventory management quality of life.

## Character Creation

### [New Character Creation Presets](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/205)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds dozens of new face presets for every race and gender combination. Significantly more variety than vanilla with unique features and expressions. An early and well-maintained mod that keeps expanding.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Character Creation
- **Notes:** Load after Faces of Faerun for maximum variety. Some presets may clip with certain hairstyles.

### [Astralities' Hair Color Supplement](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/2002)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds dozens of new hair colors including fantasy tones (pastels, vibrant shades, multi-tone highlights) beyond vanilla's realistic palette. Works with all hairstyles including Tav's Hair Salon.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Character Creation
- **Notes:** Also available as Astralities' Hair Color Collection (7852) and Overhaul (5020) for more comprehensive options.

### [Horns of Faerun](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/466)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds dozens of new horn styles for tieflings, including curved, spiral, branching, and crystalline designs. Dramatically expands tiefling customization beyond the limited vanilla options.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Character Creation
- **Notes:** Horn colors can be customized independently. Works with all vanilla and modded skin tones.

### [Basket Full of Equipment — SFW](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/87)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Massive equipment mod adding hundreds of lore-friendly armor sets, clothing, and accessories. All items are cosmetic/camp clothing or balanced equipment obtainable through a summonable chest.
- **Dependencies:** None
- **Category:** Equipment
- **Notes:** The SFW version. An NSFW version is available from the same mod page's Optional Files section, adding revealing and adult-themed outfits.

## Races

### [Fantastical Multiverse](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/215)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds dozens of new playable races from D&D 5e lore including Aasimar, Genasi, Firbolg, Kenku, Tabaxi, Tortle, Yuan-ti, and many more. Each race has unique racial traits, abilities, and dialogue tags where possible.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Races
- **Notes:** One of the most comprehensive race mods. New races may not have full NPC reactivity but racial traits and abilities are fully functional.

---

## Classes & Subclasses

### [Artificer Class and All Subclasses](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/1779)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Full implementation of the Artificer class from D&D 5e. Includes Alchemist, Artillerist, Battle Smith, and Armorer subclasses. Features infusions, magical tinkering, and tool expertise.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Classes & Subclasses
- **Notes:** One of the highest-quality class mods. Battle Smith's Steel Defender is a fully functional summoned companion.

### [Blood Hunter Class — Lycan and Ghostslayer Subclasses](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/2151)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Implementation of Matt Mercer's Blood Hunter class with Lycan and Ghostslayer subclasses. Features Blood Maledict curses and Crimson Rite weapon enhancements that damage yourself to empower attacks.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Classes & Subclasses
- **Notes:** Also see Blood Hunter Expanded (5527) which adds Profane Soul and Mutant subclasses.

---

## Spells

### [5e Spells](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/125)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds over 100 missing spells from D&D 5e rulebooks. Includes Booming Blade, Green-Flame Blade, Toll the Dead, Mind Sliver, Absorb Elements, and many more. Spells are integrated into existing spell lists.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Spells
- **Notes:** One of the most endorsed BG3 mods. Scrolls for new spells appear as random loot. Combines well with Mystra's Spells (see mod-ideas.md).

## Feats & Progression

### [Feats Extra](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/167)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds dozens of missing D&D 5e feats including Crusher, Piercer, Slasher, Fey Touched, Shadow Touched, Telekinetic, Telepathic, Metamagic Adept, Eldritch Adept, Fighting Initiate, and Gunner.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Feats & Progression
- **Notes:** Fey Touched (Misty Step + 1 free enchantment spell) is fantastic for non-casters.

### [Unlock Level Curve — Level 13-20](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/377)

- **Version:** Patch 8 (see mod page)
- **Description:** Extends the level cap from 12 to 20, unlocking higher-level class features, spell slots, and abilities. Multiclass combinations that were impossible in vanilla become viable with 20 levels.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** Feats & Progression
- **Notes:** Combine with XP multiplier mods and difficulty mods for balanced progression. The game is balanced around level 12.

## Combat & Difficulty

### [Tactician Plus](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/661)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Increases enemy HP, AC, saving throws, and damage scaling beyond Tactician and Honor Mode levels. Multiple difficulty tiers — choose how punishing you want the game to be.
- **Dependencies:** Script Extender
- **Category:** Combat & Difficulty
- **Notes:** Essential for balancing increased party sizes. Start with the lowest scaling tier and increase if it's too easy.

### [Combat Extender](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/5207)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** A comprehensive combat difficulty framework. Separately scales enemy HP, AC, attack bonus, saving throws, damage, and adds new enemy abilities. More granular than Tactician Plus with per-stat scaling.
- **Dependencies:** Script Extender, Mod Configuration Menu (9162)
- **Category:** Combat & Difficulty
- **Notes:** Use this OR Tactician Plus for stat scaling — not both. Combat Extender is more configurable but requires more setup.

---

## Gameplay Mechanics

### [Camp Event Notifications](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/1879)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds a notification icon when camp events are available, ensuring you never miss companion dialogue, romance scenes, or story progression during long rests.
- **Dependencies:** Script Extender
- **Category:** Gameplay Mechanics
- **Notes:** Many players miss dozens of camp scenes because they don't rest often enough — this mod eliminates that problem entirely.
