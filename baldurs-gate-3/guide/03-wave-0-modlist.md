# Wave 0 Modlist

## Bug Fixes & Community Patches

With Patch 8 (Hotfix #36, April 2025), BG3 is highly stable with built-in mod support. No external bug fix mods are needed for Wave 0.

---

## UI Enhancements

### [ImpUI (ImprovedUI)](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/366)

- **Version:** Patch 8 Update 3 (April 22, 2025)
- **Description:** Enhances the existing UI to improve the modding experience by removing intrusive in-game warnings and expanding character creation options. Last updated specifically for Patch 8 with re-added character creation features. Provides a cleaner, more mod-friendly interface.
- **Dependencies:** None
- **Category:** UI Enhancements
- **Notes:** Not actively maintained as of April 2025. Use only if other mods require its features. Load early — before all other UI mods.

### [Native Camera Tweaks](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/945)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Unlocks the native camera for more freedom of movement and better viewing angles. Lets you zoom in closer to characters and tilt the camera more than vanilla allows. Essential for appreciating environments and character details during exploration.
- **Dependencies:** None
- **Category:** UI Enhancements
- **Notes:** Configure hotkeys via the mod's settings file to adjust sensitivity and limits.

### [WASD Character Movement](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/781)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds WASD keyboard movement controls, replacing click-to-move with direct character control. Dramatically improves the feel of exploration for players accustomed to direct character control in third-person RPGs.
- **Dependencies:** Native Mod Loader or Script Extender
- **Category:** UI Enhancements
- **Notes:** Highly configurable — adjust movement speed, toggle between click-to-move and WASD, and set keybindings.

### [Better Hotbar 2](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/2417)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Overhauls the hotbar UI with more rows, better organization, and improved visibility of abilities and items. Reduces hotbar clutter and makes finding spells and items during combat much faster.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** UI Enhancements
- **Notes:** Essential for spellcasters who accumulate many abilities. Configurable row count and sizing.

### [Always Show Approvals](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/4675)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Displays which companion will approve or disapprove of each dialogue choice before selecting it. Takes the guesswork out of companion relationship management and helps beginners understand how their choices affect the party.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** UI Enhancements
- **Notes:** A great learning tool for understanding companion personalities. Can be toggled if you prefer the mystery.

### [Better Character and Party Panels](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/1580)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Enhances the character sheet and party panels with better organization, more visible stats, and improved layout. Makes managing your party's equipment, stats, and abilities much more intuitive for new players.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI
- **Category:** UI Enhancements
- **Notes:** Excellent for beginners learning the stat system — all relevant numbers are clearly displayed.

## Quality of Life

### [Bags Bags Bags](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/880)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds a comprehensive set of auto-sorting bags and containers to your inventory. Items are automatically organized into categories (potions, scrolls, arrows, grenades, equipment, etc.), keeping your inventory clean and searchable throughout your playthrough.
- **Dependencies:** None
- **Category:** Quality of Life
- **Notes:** Bags appear in the tutorial chest at camp. Pick them up once and they work automatically.

### [Highlight (Almost) All](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/885)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Improves the highlight/outline system so interactive objects are much more visible. Corpses, containers, and lootable items glow more distinctly, making it much harder to miss loot and interactable objects in dark environments.
- **Dependencies:** None
- **Category:** Quality of Life
- **Notes:** Essential for thorough exploration — vanilla highlights can be very subtle.

### [All-In-One Collector Total (Auto Loot)](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/17089)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Automatically collects all nearby loot from corpses, containers, and the environment at the press of a button. Eliminates the tedious process of clicking every body after combat. Highly configurable with filters for what to collect.
- **Dependencies:** Script Extender
- **Category:** Quality of Life
- **Notes:** Can be configured to only activate during exploration, not combat. Set a reasonable radius to avoid over-collecting.

### [FTSortingBags](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/22369)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** An alternative or complement to Bags Bags Bags — provides automatic inventory sorting with labeled containers that organize items by type. Works seamlessly to keep your inventory manageable as you accumulate items.
- **Dependencies:** None
- **Category:** Quality of Life
- **Notes:** Use alongside or instead of Bags Bags Bags depending on preference. Both are verified compatible with Patch 8.

## Lightweight Graphics

### [Tav's Hair Salon](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/213)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds a massive collection of new hairstyles for all races and body types. Dramatically expands character creation options without changing gameplay. One of the most popular BG3 mods with thousands of endorsements.
- **Dependencies:** ImprovedUI (for full character creator integration)
- **Category:** Lightweight Graphics
- **Notes:** Hair physics work correctly in-game. New styles added regularly by the author.

### [Faces of Faerun](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/429)

- **Version:** Latest (see mod page)
- **Description:** Adds a large collection of high-quality face presets for all races and body types. Provides much more variety than vanilla faces with better textures and more distinctive, lore-friendly features.
- **Dependencies:** None
- **Category:** Lightweight Graphics
- **Notes:** Faces blend naturally with vanilla body models. Pure visual improvement with no gameplay impact.
