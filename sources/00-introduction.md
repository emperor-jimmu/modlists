# Introduction

## Purpose

This guide provides a curated, tested collection of mods for **Euro Truck Simulator 2** (ETS2). Every mod listed here has been verified for compatibility with game version **1.60** and configured to work together as a cohesive modlist. Whether you are returning after a break or building a modded profile from scratch, this guide serves as a reliable reference for assembling a stable, visually rich, and immersive experience.

## Mod Sourcing Philosophy

No mods in this guide are sourced from the Steam Workshop. Workshop subscriptions introduce silent updates, broken savegames from forced upgrades, and a lack of control over version pinning. Instead, every mod is downloaded from trusted third-party sites where version history and archival copies are available:

- [ets2world.com](https://ets2world.com)
- [ets2mods.lt](https://ets2mods.lt)
- [ets2.lt/en](https://ets2.lt/en)
- [modland.net](https://modland.net)
- [truckymods.io](https://truckymods.io)
- [modhub.us](https://modhub.us)
- [terramaps.net](https://terramaps.net)
- [promods.net](https://promods.net)
- [forum.scssoft.com](https://forum.scssoft.com)

These sites are chosen for their community reputation, uptime, and resistance to takedown pressure. All mods linked from this guide have been scanned for malware and tested for game-breaking issues at the time of writing.

## Goals

This modlist aims to enhance ETS2 across five pillars:

1. **Better Graphics** — Reshade presets, weather packs, texture overhauls, and lighting tweaks that bring the game closer to photorealism at a 4K baseline resolution.
2. **More Trucks** — Unofficial truck brands and high-quality reworks of SCS trucks with detailed interiors, custom sounds, and realistic physics.
3. **More Maps** — Map combo compatibility with ProMods, RusMap, and other major map mods that expand the playable area across Europe, Asia, and beyond.
4. **Gameplay Depth** — Economy rebalances, realistic company management, traffic density, and AI behaviour improvements that add complexity without grinding.
5. **Role-Playing** — Mods that support immersive driving: realistic fuel consumption, weight stations, parking enforcement, sleep cycles, and CB radio simulation.

## What This Guide Is Not

This guide **explicitly excludes** cheating or overpowered mods. You will not find:

- Money or XP cheats
- Instant-unlock garages or skills
- Physics-defying trucks with 5000 HP engines
- Teleportation or delivery-skip mods

The goal is a challenging, rewarding simulation — not a shortcut to max level. If you want to trivialise the game, this is not the guide for you.

## Baseline Resolution

All visual mods in this guide are selected and tested at **3840 × 2160 (4K)**. If you are playing at 1080p or 1440p, some texture packs will still improve quality, but you may need to downscale certain 4K-only assets. Performance impact notes at each entry account for scaling.

## Guide Structure

The guide is organised into the following sections:

1. **Introduction** (this file) — Scope, philosophy, and goals.
2. **Core Framework** — Essential mods that every profile needs: map connectors, required libraries, load order foundation.
3. **Graphics & Weather** — Reshade, weather packs, lighting, skyboxes, and texture overhauls.
4. **Trucks** — New truck brands, truck reworks, sound packs, and tuning parts.
5. **Trailers & Cargo** — New trailers, cargo packs, and trailer skin packs.
6. **Maps** — Map mods and connectors for a full map combo.
7. **Gameplay & Realism** — Economy, physics, AI, traffic, and company mods.
8. **UI & QoL** — Dashboard computers, navigation enhancements, menu tweaks.
9. **Sound** — Engine packs, environment audio, and horn packs.
10. **Traffic & AI** — AI traffic packs, police mods, and real company skins.
11. **Appendices** — Load order template, troubleshooting, and site index.

Each mod entry follows a consistent format:

## Mod Name
- **Version:** Latest tested version
- **Source:** Download URL
- **Category:** Section category
**Description:** What the mod does and why it is included.
**Compatibility:** Known conflicts, dependencies, and load order placement notes.
**Performance Impact:** Estimated FPS cost and VRAM usage at 4K.
**Configuration:** In-mod settings, if any, required for correct behaviour.
