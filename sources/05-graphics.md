## ReShade Presets (Community)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Version** | Various |
| **Source** | SCS Forum, TruckyMods |
| **Type** | Post-processing |

Third-party ReShade injectors with custom presets for ETS2:
- **Realistic Color Preset** — neutralizes SCS oversaturation, adds filmic tonemapping
- **Cinematic ETS2** — adds vignette, film grain, deeper blacks
- **NVIDIA Freestyle Alternatives** — sharpening, contrast, detail enhancement

Free to use. Requires ReShade 5.x+ installation.

**Compatibility:** 1.60, conflicts with in-game color settings and some lighting mods

**Performance Impact at 4K:** ~5-10% FPS loss (~3-6 FPS) depending on effects enabled

### Recommendation

For a 4K build with an RTX 4070 or better: **Nextgen Graphics** + **Brutal Traffic** pair well. For lower-end hardware, **Project Next Gen** alone provides a solid visual upgrade at minimal cost. Avoid stacking multiple reshade presets or two comprehensive graphics overhauls together.
