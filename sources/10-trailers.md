### Notes

- **Jazzycat packs:** The main Trailers and Cargo Pack and the Overweight Trailers and Cargo Pack are mutually exclusive — the overweight pack is a subset of the main pack. Use one or the other, not both. The Military Cargo Pack is standalone and compatible with either.
- **Total size:** A full trailer setup (Jazzycat main + Military + TrafficManiac + TZ Express) exceeds 5 GB. Ensure you have sufficient disk space and VRAM (8 GB+ recommended).
- **TZ Express stability:** Test this pack on a new profile before adding to an existing save. The reported car transporter crash may affect some configurations.
- **Load order:** Place cargo/trailer mods above map mods in the load order. AI traffic packs go in the AI/traffic section of the load order. Enhanced SCS Cargo should be above other trailer mods.
- **AI traffic packs:** If using TrafficManiac's trailer pack with Clean Traffic — No Default SCS Trailers, place Clean Traffic above the TrafficManiac pack. Consider whether you want to see both addon AND default traffic trailers.
- **SiSL's Trailers Pack v1.59:** SiSL (of Mega Pack fame) also released a trailer pack with 700+ real company skins, but it targets 1.59. An update for 1.60 may be released — check [ets2.lt](https://ets2.lt/en/sisls-trailers-pack-v1-59/) for updates.
