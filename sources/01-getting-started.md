# Getting Started

## Buying Euro Truck Simulator 2

ETS2 is available on Steam. The base game alone is playable, but the guide assumes you own all current map DLCs. ProMods — the cornerstone of the map section in this guide — requires the following DLCs to function:

| DLC | Required By |
|-----|-------------|
| Going East! | ProMods (base requirement) |
| Scandinavia | ProMods (base requirement) |
| Vive la France! | ProMods (base requirement) |
| Italia | ProMods (base requirement) |
| Beyond the Baltic Sea | ProMods (base requirement) |
| Iberia | ProMods (base requirement) |
| West Balkans | ProMods (base requirement) |

These are often available in discounted bundles during Steam sales. If you are missing any, the ProMods map will show greyed-out sectors or fail to load entirely.

Optional but recommended: the **Cargo Pack** DLCs (Heavy Cargo Pack, Special Transport, Krone Trailer Pack, Feldbinder Trailer Pack) add cargo variety that pairs well with the gameplay and trailer mods later in this guide.

## Basic Controls & Gameplay Loop

### Controls

The default controls are keyboard + mouse. Most players eventually upgrade to a steering wheel, but the game is fully playable without one:

| Action | Default Key |
|--------|-------------|
| Accelerate | W or Up Arrow |
| Brake / Reverse | S or Down Arrow |
| Steer Left / Right | A / D or Left / Right Arrow |
| Indicator (Left / Right) | Left Bracket / Right Bracket |
| Headlights | L |
| Horn | H |
| Wipers | P |
| Parking Brake | Spacebar |
| N | Map / GPS |
| Enter / Exit Vehicle | E |
| Shift + Up Arrow | Cruise Control + |
| Shift + Down Arrow | Cruise Control - |
| F2 | Dashboard display toggle |
| F5 | Navigation mode toggle |
| F6 | Jobs menu |
| F8 | Info / messages |

You can rebind every key from the Options → Controls menu. If you have a controller or wheel, bindings are detected automatically.

### The Gameplay Loop

The core loop is simple and addictive:

1. **Find a job** — Browse the Freight Market or Cargo Market from the jobs menu (F6). Quick Jobs are available if you do not own a trailer yet.
2. **Drive to the pickup** — Navigate to the depot using the GPS (N for map, F5 for route overview).
3. **Hook up the trailer** — Park under the trailer and press T to attach.
4. **Deliver the cargo** — Drive to the destination within the time limit. Damage to the trailer reduces your pay.
5. **Get paid** — Earnings go toward buying your own truck, expanding your garage, and hiring drivers.
6. **Repeat or upgrade** — Use your earnings to buy trucks, hire employees, and grow your company.

As you level up, you unlock new skills (long-distance driving, hazardous materials, fragile cargo) that open higher-paying jobs.

## Role-Playing: Define Your Story

This guide treats ETS2 as more than a driving game. The mechanics support a rich role-playing experience if you approach it with intention. Before building a modded profile, decide who you want to be on the road.

### Owner-Operator vs. Fleet Owner

| Role | Playstyle |
|------|-----------|
| **Owner-Operator** | You own one truck. You drive every delivery yourself. You care about fuel economy, route efficiency, and the details of your rig. Slower progression, but every upgrade matters. |
| **Fleet Owner** | You build a company. Hire drivers, buy multiple trucks, expand garages across Europe. You still drive, but your income comes from managing a team. Faster money, more admin. |

There is no wrong choice. You can start as an owner-operator and grow into a fleet owner over time.

### Naming Your Company

Your company name appears on your truck doors, your trailers, and the delivery screen. Choose something that fits your character:

- **Realistic:** A logistics company name (Nordic Freight, Adriatic Logistics, Balkan Express)
- **Personal:** Your name or initials (Nimro Trans, Mike's Haulage)
- **Thematic:** Based on your home region or style (Alpine Transport, Mediterranean Shipping)
- **Minimalist:** A single word (Horizon, Apex, Convoy)

### Choosing Your Home Garage

The game prompts you to choose a home garage when you create a profile. This determines your starting city and the region where you find early jobs. Considerations:

- **Central Europe** (Germany, Netherlands, Belgium) — Short, dense routes. Great for learning.
- **United Kingdom** — Left-side driving. A fun challenge after you have the basics.
- **Scandinavia** — Longer routes, toll roads, ferries. Beautiful but spread out.
- **Iberia or Italy** — Good mix of motorways and regional roads. Warmer climates.
- **Eastern Europe** (Poland, Czechia) — Cheaper garages, good for early game budget.

Pick a city you enjoy driving through on the map screen. You can buy additional garages later.

### Role-Playing Prompts

To get into character on every trip:

- **Pre-trip inspection:** Check your tyres, lights, and damage before leaving the depot.
- **Log your deliveries:** Keep a simple text file or spreadsheet with date, route, cargo, and pay.
- **Stick to realistic hours:** Use the fatigue simulation. Stop for the night when your driver gets tired.
- **Fuel strategy:** Fill up only when fuel is below a quarter tank. Compare prices on the GPS.
- **Avoid teleporting:** Never skip a delivery or teleport to a garage. Drive every kilometre.
- **Listen to in-game radio:** Tune into real internet radio stations from the country you are driving through.
- **Commit to the load:** Once you accept a job, deliver it. No cancelling for a better-paying load.

## Profile Management: Keep Modded and Vanilla Separate

ETS2 supports multiple profiles. Creating separate profiles for modded and vanilla playthroughs is essential for two reasons:

1. **Savegame corruption:** Disabling a mod mid-playthrough can break a save permanently. A dedicated modded profile ensures you never accidentally load a modded save in vanilla.
2. **Load order experimentation:** When testing new mods, you want a profile that is already set up for the full modlist. If something breaks, your vanilla profile is untouched.

### Recommendation

- **Profile 1 — Vanilla:** Unmodded. For achievement hunting, testing performance baselines, and playing while waiting for mod updates.
- **Profile 2 — Modded:** The full modlist from this guide. Never load this profile without the correct mods enabled.
- **Profile 3 (optional) — Test:** A throwaway profile for experimenting with new mods before adding them to your main modded profile.

Name them clearly: "Vanilla," "Modded — ProMods," "Modded — Test." This avoids accidental loads and saves you from hours of troubleshooting.

When creating a modded profile in the game's profile menu, give it a distinctive name and select the **Modded** checkbox. The game will remind you every time you load it if mods are missing or changed — this is a feature, not a bug.
