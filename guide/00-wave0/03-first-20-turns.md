# First 20 Turns — Liu Bei Campaign Guide

This guide walks you through the first 20 turns as **Liu Bei** on **Normal** difficulty. Liu Bei starts in the northeast with strong generals (Guan Yu, Zhang Fei) and excellent diplomatic options — the ideal learning faction.

## Turn 1

### Diplomacy
- Send a **Trade Agreement** offer to every faction you've met (Gongsun Zan, Han Sui, Kong Rong, etc.). Accept any that come back. Trade income is small early but compounds.
- Check if any faction will give you money for food. Offer 10-20 food per turn for a lump sum. Use that gold to build.

### Army
- Merge damaged units. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei start with good retinues — keep them together.
- Move your main army toward **Dong Commandery** (the settlement just south). Do not attack yet — get into position.

### Buildings
- **Commandery capital (Pingyuan):** Build the first tier of a **Farm** (food production) or **Market** (income). Prioritize food if you're at or below 0 surplus.

### Characters
- Check your court. Assign any idle characters as **Assignments** — "+10 satisfaction" assignments on your faction leader or heir prevent loyalty problems.
- Set your heir if one isn't assigned. Liu Bei as faction leader, Guan Yu as heir is a strong start.

## Turn 2

### Army
- Continue moving toward Dong Commandery. If it's unguarded, you can take it next turn without a fight (AI will usually retreat).
- Recruit 1-2 militia units in Pingyuan if you have spare income. Ji Militia (spear) are cheap and effective.

### Diplomacy
- Check for **Non-Aggression Pacts** with your neighbors. Gongsun Zan to the north is a useful friend — he'll occupy Yuan Shao's attention.
- Offer NAPs for free or for a small payment. Most factions will accept early on.

### Buildings
- If you built a farm last turn, start a **Market** or **Workshop** in your capital now. Diversify income.
- Build a **Road** in your capital commandery. Roads unlock trade routes and speed up army movement.

## Turn 3-5: Secure Your First Province

### Conquest
- Attack Dong Commandery. If the enemy army is nearby, fight manually — you outnumber them with Guan Yu + Zhang Fei.
- In battle: use Guan Yu and Zhang Fei aggressively against enemy generals. Their dueling skills are excellent. Protect your militia line while the heroes flank.
- After capturing Dong, move to **Puyang** or **Taishan** — your second province target.

### Economy
- Build farms in every new settlement. Food surplus = public order = growth.
- Keep at least one building slot for public order (temples or garrisons).

### Diplomacy
- By now, one neighboring faction may have declared war. Don't panic — they usually want a weak settlement. Offer a small payment for peace if you're not ready.
- Seek a **Coalition** or **Military Alliance** with Gongsun Zan or Kong Rong. A single ally deters multiple enemies.

## Turn 6-10: Expand Carefully

### Conquest Strategy
- Target **one direction only**. Don't fight on two fronts. If you're at war with Yuan Shao to the north and陶 Qian to the south, make peace with one.
- Capture your second full commandery. This gives you a second building slot for income or recruitment.
- Do NOT take settlements you can't defend. Leave buffer territory between you and strong enemies.

### Army Management
- You should have one main army (Liu Bei + Guan Yu + Zhang Fei). Do not hire a second general yet — the upkeep cost is too high.
- If a second enemy army appears, recruit a **garrison** in your capital (cheap militia). Don't create a second full army until you have two commanderies fully developed.

### Economy Check (End of Turn 10)
- Food surplus: +5 minimum. If below, build more farms.
- Income: +500 minimum per turn. If below, build markets, sign more trade deals.
- Public order: All settlements above -5. Assign characters with "+public order" skills as administrators.

### Reforms
- Research **Agricultural Reforms** (food) or **Taxation Reforms** (income) first. Military reforms can wait.

## Turn 11-15: Stabilise & Prepare

### Buildings
- Upgrade your capital to **Town** level (population 4) if you have the gold. This unlocks second-tier buildings and a garrison slot.
- Build a **Temple** or **Confucian School** in settlements with unrest. Public order is the cap on expansion speed.

### Characters & Satisfaction
- Check every character's satisfaction. If anyone is below 60, give them an **Ancillary** (item), a **Court Position**, or an **Administrator** role.
- Recruit one or two new characters if you have open retinue slots. Look for characters with "+commerce" or "-corruption" traits.

### Diplomacy Targets
- Identify your **first mid-game enemy**. On Normal, this is likely Yuan Shao (north) or Cao Cao (southwest).
- Build spy networks. Send a character with high cunning as a spy into your next target's court.

## Turn 16-20: The First Crisis

### What to Expect
- Around turn 15-20, your nearest major power will declare war or demand tribute. This is normal.
- Your public order may dip as your empire grows. Assign administrators and build temples.
- A random event (dilemma) will test your decision-making. Choose options that favour food or satisfaction.

### Military Action
- If at war, fight defensively. Let the enemy attack your settlements so you fight on favourable terrain.
- In battle: use your generals to kill routing enemies (increases army XP). Keep the main force together.
- Consider recruiting a second army only if:
  - You have +10 food surplus
  - You have +1000 gold in reserve
  - You need to defend a second front

### Goals Achieved by Turn 20
- 2-3 commanderies captured and stable
- Positive food and income
- At least one ally or coalition
- Core army: Liu Bei + Guan Yu + Zhang Fei, at least rank 3 each
- One spy placed in a rival court

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **Bankrupt?** Disband the most expensive unit(s). Cancel all diplomacy payments. Sell food for gold.
- **Attacked on two fronts?** Make peace with one side — pay them if needed. Your priority is to survive, not to win every war.
- **General assassinated?** Recruit a replacement from the pool. Any Vanguard or Commander can fill Guan Yu's role temporarily.
- **Revolts?** Lower taxes in the rebellious province. Build a temple. Assign a character with "+public order" there.
