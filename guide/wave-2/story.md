# Wave 2 — Beyond the Surface

You wake up for the third time.

The forest is gone. The slimes are gone. Even the Guide is gone. There is only you, and a voice that is not a voice — a presence that has been watching since the beginning.

*You have returned. Again. I wondered if you would.*

The world is broken. Reality fractures at the edges. Ancient things stir in the deep places — things that were old when the first slime crawled from the dirt. They are not your enemies. They are your teachers.

*You have conquered the surface. You have conquered the depths. But you have not conquered yourself.*

The voice does not offer guidance. It offers questions. Why do you fight? What do you seek? Is power the goal, or is mastery?

*Choose your path. Not the path of least resistance, but the path of most intention. Prove yourself not by what you wield, but by what you choose not to wield.*

The challenges ahead are not imposed by the world. They are imposed by you. A warrior who refuses the sword. A builder who refuses the craft. A survivor who refuses the second chance.

*The surface was never the destination. The destination was always this: the moment you realize there is no surface. Only depth. Only choice. Only the question of what you do when no one is watching.*

The voice falls silent. It does not leave. It never leaves. But it will not speak again until you have proven your answer.

The world waits. The ancient things wait. And somewhere, in a place that is not a place, the Guide smiles.

*He remembers now.*
