## Why New American?

A large-scale US map purpose-built for industrial farming:

- **89 fields** (65 agricultural + rice) + 6 forest areas
- **11 production points** — extensive built-in production infrastructure
- **4 selling points + BGA** — full economic loop
- **Precision Farming compatible** — designed for smart farming
- **Custom terrains with GPS-friendly slopes** — optimized for large equipment
- **Second farm area** (field 79) — expand to dual-farm operations
