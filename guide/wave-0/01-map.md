## Why Oak Bridge Farm?

Oxygendavid's successor to the legendary Calmsden Farm. A compact UK map designed for small-scale, realistic farming:

- **36 fields** with missions — manageable scale for a beginner
- **5 sell points + BGA + sawmill** — all the economic options without overwhelming choice
- **Custom lighting and seasonal visuals** — atmospheric without needing additional visual mods
- **Tight lanes and hedgerows** — forces careful driving and realistic small-equipment use
- **Built-in collectables and custom sounds** — world feels lived-in from day one

The internal filename is `FS25_calmsden.zip` confirming its Calmsden heritage.
