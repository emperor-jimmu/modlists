## Not Available

The following popular FS22 QoL mods have **not been ported** to FS25 as of July 2026:

| Mod | Status |
|---|---|
| **Follow Me** | No FS25 version exists. Decker_MMIV has not ported from FS22. |
| **Enhanced Vehicle HUD** (VCA) | No FS25 version. This FS22 staple (RPM, fuel, speed display) is a notable gap. |
| **CoursePlay** | Exists (ModHub #331515, GitHub, v8.1.0.3) but classified as **gameplay-altering**. Deferred to Wave 1. |

## Wave 0 Recommended Settings

- **Economic Difficulty**: Normal
- **Plant Growth**: Normal (not fast — learn the rhythm)
- **Fuel Usage**: On
- **Traffic**: On
- **Auto-Repair**: Off (experience the cost of neglect)
- **Seasons**: On (learn crop timing from the start)
