## Arena Building

A good arena makes boss fights 50% easier. Here's how to build one:

**Basic Arena Structure:**
- **Size:** 200×100 blocks minimum for most bosses
- **Platforms:** 3-5 rows of platforms, spaced 15-20 blocks apart vertically
- **Lighting:** Torches every 20 blocks (prevents enemy spawns in some cases)
- **Campfire:** Place every 50 blocks for health regen
- **Heart Lantern:** Place every 50 blocks for additional healing
- **Bast Statue:** Place every 30 blocks for defense boost
- **Sunflower:** Place on the ground to reduce enemy spawn rate

**Class-Specific Arena Features:**
- **Melee:** Narrow corridors with platforms for vertical movement. Add heart statues for emergency healing.
- **Ranged:** Long horizontal platforms for kiting. Add minecart tracks for mobility.
- **Magic:** Open space for projectile weapons. Add mana stars and heart crystals.
- **Summoner:** Large open area for minions to roam. Add plenty of platforms for you to dodge.

**Boss-Specific Arenas:**
- **Eye of Cthulhu:** Long horizontal platform (300+ blocks). No vertical obstacles.
- **Eater of Worlds:** Box arena with a hole at the bottom. Use piercing weapons.
- **Skeletron:** Long platform (200+ blocks). Keep moving.
- **Wall of Flesh:** 500+ block bridge across Hell. Use a minecart for speed.
- **Plantera:** 200×200 block open arena in the jungle. Clear all vines and plants.
- **Moon Lord:** Large open arena (300×300). Add heart lanterns and campfires everywhere.

## Farming Strategies

### Mob Farms

**Slime Farm:**
- Build a statue farm with Slime Statues connected to a 1-second timer.
- Place a lava trap at the bottom to kill slimes automatically.
- Collect gel and slime drops.

**Coin Farm (Pirate Invasion):**
- Build a large arena with heart lanterns and campfires.
- Use a Pirate Map (from enemies in the Ocean) to summon a Pirate Invasion.
- Kill pirates for coins and rare drops (Coin Gun, Golden Furniture).

### Biome Keys

Biome keys drop from enemies in specific biomes during Hardmode:
- **Jungle Key** — Jungle enemies (0.04% drop rate)
- **Corruption Key** — Corruption enemies
- **Crimson Key** — Crimson enemies
- **Hallow Key** — Hallow enemies
- **Frozen Key** — Snow biome enemies
- **Desert Key** — Desert enemies

**Tip:** Use Water Candles and Battle Potions to increase spawn rates. Farm during events (Pumpkin Moon, Frost Moon) for faster kills.

### Rod of Discord

The Rod of Discord drops from Chaos Elementals in the Underground Hallow (0.2% drop rate).

**Strategy:**
- Build an arena in the Underground Hallow with platforms.
- Use Water Candles and Battle Potions.
- Kill Chaos Elementals consistently. Expect 500+ kills.

### Boss Summon Item Farming

**Suspicious Looking Eye (Eye of Cthulhu):**
- Craft with 6 Lens at a Demon/Crimson Altar.
- Lens drop from Demon Eyes (common at night).

**Boss Summons (Hardmode):**
- **Mechanical Bosses:** Craft with Souls + Bars at a Mythril/Orichalcum Anvil.
- **Plantera:** Break Plantera's Bulb in the Underground Jungle.
- **Golem:** Break Lihzahrd Altars in the Jungle Temple.

## Wiring & Teleporters

### Basic Logic Gates

**Logic Gates:**
- **Logic Gate Lamp (On/Off):** Input signals
- **Logic Gate (AND/OR):** Combine signals
- **Logic Gate (NOT):** Invert signal

**Example: Automatic Door:**
1. Place a pressure plate at the door entrance.
2. Connect it to a Logic Gate Lamp (On).
3. Connect the lamp to an actuator on the door.
4. Step on the plate → door opens.

### Teleporter Hub Networks

**Setup:**
1. Craft Teleporters (10 Wire + 5 Iron/Lead Bars at Iron Anvil).
2. Place teleporters in key locations (base, jungle, dungeon, hell).
3. Connect each pair with Wire.
4. Step on a teleporter to travel to the linked one.

**Tips:**
- Use different colored wires for different routes (Red, Green, Blue).
- Label teleporters with signs or paintings.
- Build a central hub with teleporters to all major biomes.

### Actuator-Based Farms

**Example: Auto Fishing Farm:**
1. Build a pool of water (300+ tiles).
2. Place a Fishing Rod on a pressure plate.
3. Use actuators to open/close a gate.
4. When you step on the plate, the gate opens, the rod casts, and you catch fish automatically.

## Advanced NPC Housing

### Pylon Network

Pylons allow teleportation between biomes. Each biome needs a pylon and 2 NPCs.

**Recommended Pylon Locations:**
- **Forest Pylon** — Near your main base
- **Snow Pylon** — In the snow biome
- **Desert Pylon** — In the desert
- **Jungle Pylon** — In the jungle
- **Hallow Pylon** — In the Hallow (Hardmode)
- **Ocean Pylon** — Near the ocean

**Setup:**
1. Build housing for 2 NPCs in each biome.
2. Buy the Pylon from the Merchant (after 2 NPCs live in the biome).
3. Place the Pylon and interact with it to teleport.

### Biome-Specific Housing

**NPC Preferences:**
- **Nurse** — Prefers Forest biome, likes living near the Doctor.
- **Arms Dealer** — Prefers Desert biome, dislikes the Jungle.
- **Dryad** — Prefers Jungle biome, dislikes the Desert.
- **Wizard** — Prefers Hallow biome, likes living near the Truffle.

**Tip:** Use the Housing UI to check NPC happiness. Happy NPCs sell pylons and offer discounts.

### Happiness Optimization

NPC happiness affects prices and pylon availability:
- **Love:** +10% price reduction, sells pylon
- **Like:** +5% price reduction
- **Dislike:** +5% price increase
- **Hate:** +10% price increase, won't sell pylon

**Strategy:**
- Place NPCs in their preferred biomes.
- Place NPCs near others they like.
- Avoid crowding (more than 3 NPCs in one area reduces happiness).

## Biome Spread Management

In Hardmode, Corruption/Crimson and Hallow spread aggressively. Here's how to manage it:

### Pre-Hardmode Preparation

**Trenching:**
- Dig 3-block-wide trenches around your base and key biomes.
- Line the trenches with non-corruptible blocks (wood, clay, ash).
- This prevents spread until you get the Clentaminator.

**Hellevator:**
- Dig a 3-block-wide shaft from the surface to the Underworld.
- Line it with non-corruptible blocks.
- This serves as a firebreak and a fast travel route.

### Hardmode Management

**Clentaminator:**
- Buy from the Steampunker (after defeating Plantera).
- Use Green Solution to purify Corruption/Crimson.
- Use Blue Solution to spread Hallow.
- Use Purple/Red Solution to spread Corruption/Crimson (if desired).

**Strategy:**
- Purify a large area around your base first.
- Create firebreaks (3-block-wide trenches) to contain spread.
- Use the Clentaminator regularly to maintain purified zones.
- Consider using the Terraformer (post-Moon Lord) for large-scale purification.
