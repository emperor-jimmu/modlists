## Why Le Mechet?

A purpose-built French vineyard map designed around wine production:

- **12 hectares of established vines** — start producing from day one
- **Traditional farmhouse and stone cellar** — atmospheric setting for the winery story
- **Companion production pack** by the same author (Ma7) adds industry buildings
- **June 2026 release** — recent, likely v1.20.0.0 compatible
