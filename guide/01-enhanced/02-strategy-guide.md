## Build Theory Fundamentals

Every build in The Witcher 3 fits one of three archetypes:

### Combat-Focused
Maximize fast attack damage, critical hit chance, and critical hit damage. Use adrenaline for powerful finishers. Best for players who enjoy active swordplay.

### Sign-Focused
Maximize Sign intensity to control the battlefield. Alternate Sign modes offer unique advantages (Yrden turret, Igni stream). Best for tactical players.

### Alchemy-Focused
Maximize toxicity to run multiple decoctions simultaneously. Decoctions provide passive bonuses (health regen, damage reduction, stamina regen). Best for players who prepare strategically.

## Hybrid Builds

The most powerful builds combine two trees:

**Combat + Alchemy (Euphoria)** -- The Euphoria mutation converts your toxicity into bonus attack power and Sign intensity. With 3+ decoctions active, you deal massive damage. Widely considered the strongest build in the game.

**Sign + Combat (Piercing Cold)** -- The Piercing Cold mutation makes Aard freeze enemies solid, allowing instant kills. Combined with sword damage against frozen enemies.

**Alchemy + Signs (Conductors of Magic)** -- Boosts Sign intensity based on your sword's damage. With alchemy-enhanced swords, your Signs hit harder than a pure Sign build.
