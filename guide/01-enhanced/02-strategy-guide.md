## Build Theory

Every witcher build falls into one of three archetypes:

### Combat Focus
- Max fast attack damage
- Critical hit chance/damage
- Adrenaline generation
- **Best for**: Players who enjoy active swordplay

### Sign Focus
- Max sign intensity
- Alternate sign modes
- Stamina regeneration
- **Best for**: Players who prefer tactical magic use

### Alchemy Focus
- Max toxicity threshold
- Potion duration and effectiveness
- Bomb damage and radius
- **Best for**: Players who preparate strategically

## Hybrid Builds

Most effective builds combine two trees:
- **Combat + Alchemy** - highest DPS potential (Euphoria mutation)
- **Sign + Combat** - balanced offense (Piercing Cold mutation)
- **Alchemy + Signs** - tactical control (Conductors of Magic)
