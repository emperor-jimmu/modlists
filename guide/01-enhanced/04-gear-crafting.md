## Witcher Gear Sets

| Set | School | Bonus | Best For |
|-----|--------|-------|----------|
| Griffin | School of the Griffin | Sign intensity | Sign builds |
| Cat (Feline) | School of the Cat | Attack power, crit | Combat builds |

## Grandmaster Crafting

Each set has a Grandmaster tier (requires Blood and Wine DLC):
- 3-piece and 6-piece set bonuses
- Crafted at Grandmaster Smith in Beauclair
- Requires rare materials (blood, hides, monster parts)

## Runewords & Glyphwords

Unlocked via "Enchantment: Start" quest (Hearts of Stone DLC):
| Runeword | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| Levity | Heavy armor counts as light |
| Entanglement | Yrden traps follow you |
| Severance | Increased Whirl/Rend range |
| Preservation | Gear never degrades |
| Invigoration | Heal above max with potions |
| Prolongation | Potions last longer on kill |
