## Witcher Gear Sets

Each School offers a complete armor and weapon set with set bonuses at Grandmaster tier:

| School | Weight | Primary Bonus | Best For |
|--------|--------|---------------|----------|
| Griffin | Medium | +Sign Intensity | Sign builds |
| Cat (Feline) | Light | +Attack Power, +Crit | Combat builds |
| Bear (Ursine) | Heavy | +Adrenaline, +Defense | Tank builds |
| Wolf | Medium | +Mixed stats | Hybrid builds |
| Viper | Light | +Poison immunity | Story / NG+ |
| Manticore | Medium | +Alchemy bonuses | Alchemy builds |

Witcher gear diagrams are found via Scavenger Hunt quests. Buy the maps from blacksmiths and armorers to start these quests.

## Grandmaster Crafting (Blood and Wine)

The Grandmaster Smith in Beauclair crafts the highest tier. Each set gains two bonuses:

**Griffin 3-pc:** Yrden trap size +40%. **6-pc:** Cast Signs while dodging.
**Feline 3-pc:** 20% chance to bleed on crit. **6-pc:** Attack power +20% for 3s after killing an enemy.
**Ursine 3-pc:** 15% damage reduction. **6-pc:** Adrenaline not consumed when taking damage.
**Wolf 3-pc:** Critical hit damage +25%. **6-pc:** All bombs deal +25% damage.
**Manticore 3-pc:** Critical hit chance +10%. **6-pc:** Bomb damage +100%.

## Runewords & Glyphwords (Hearts of Stone)

The Runewright in Upper Novigrad engraves special enchantments. Unlocking all three tiers costs 35,000 crowns.

| Runeword | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| Levity | Heavy armor counts as Light |
| Severance | Increases Whirl and Rend range by 2 |
| Preservation | Gear never degrades |
| Invigoration | Healing above max vitality increases damage |
| Prolongation | Potion effects extend on kill |
| Entanglement | Yrden traps follow you |
