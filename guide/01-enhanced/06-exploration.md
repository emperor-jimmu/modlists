# Exploration

<span class="wave-badge wave-1">Wave 1</span>

## World Map Regions

### White Orchard (Tutorial Zone)
- Fully explore before leaving (missable content)
- Places of Power: 6 total
- Gwent cards: 4+
- Hidden treasure: Temerian armor set

### Velen / Novigrad
- Largest region in the game
- ? locations: 200+ (many smuggler caches in water)
- Bandit camps, monster nests, abandoned sites
- Hidden quests at discovered locations

### Skellige
- Archipelago of 6+ islands
- Underwater treasures (lots of sailing)
- High-level monsters (level 30+ in some areas)
- Places of Power on remote islands

### Toussaint (Blood and Wine)
- Endgame zone
- Highest level content
- Hanse bases for farming
- New Gwent cards

## Treasure Hunt Tips

- Check every notice board for treasure map pins
- Witcher gear scavenger hunts appear automatically
- Hidden treasure POIs often have relic gear
- Some treasures are level-scaled to when you find them
