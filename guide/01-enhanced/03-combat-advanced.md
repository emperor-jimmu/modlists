## Enemy Weaknesses in Depth

### Necrophages (drowners, ghouls, rotfiends, wraiths)
- **Weak to**: Fire (Igni), Necrophage oil
- **Dodge pattern**: Telegraphed swipes
- **Tips**: Yrden slows them. Drowners emerge from water -- use Aard to knock them back

### Specters (noonwraiths, nightwraiths, plagues)
- **Weak to**: Moon Dust bombs (prevents phasing), Specter oil
- **Dodge pattern**: Teleporting, unseeable attacks
- **Tips**: Yrden makes them tangible. Fight during the day to reduce their power

### Ogroids (trolls, gargoyles, golems, cyclops)
- **Weak to**: Northern Wind bombs, Ogroid oil
- **Dodge pattern**: Slow but powerful telegraphed swings
- **Tips**: Aard interrupts their charges. Fight in open areas

### Beasts (wolves, bears, panthers, werewolves)
- **Weak to**: Grapeshot bombs, Beast oil
- **Dodge pattern**: Lunges and bites
- **Tips**: Axii stuns them briefly. Werewolves regenerate -- use Moon Dust or Igni

### Draconids (wyverns, forktails, basilisks, cockatrices)
- **Weak to**: Draconid oil
- **Tips**: Crossbow shots knock flying draconids to the ground for massive follow-up damage

### Elementals (golems, elementals, ice giants)
- **Weak to**: Elementa oil
- **Tips**: Igni deals bonus damage. Their attacks are slow but hit hard -- dodge, don't parry

## Advanced Techniques

### Countering
Tap parry just before enemy attack lands. Staggers humanoid enemies for follow-up. Does not work on most monsters.

### Rend (Heavy Attack Skill)
Hold heavy attack to charge. Consumes stamina and adrenaline. Armor-piercing, high damage. Best against single armored targets.

### Whirl (Light Attack Skill)
Hold light attack to spin in place. Hits multiple enemies. Consumes stamina continuously. Devastating with Severance runeword.

### Potion Management
Always have these potions available:
- **Swallow** -- health regeneration (your primary healing potion)
- **Thunderbolt** -- +30% attack power during storms
- **Cat** -- night vision in dark caves
- **White Raffard's Decoction** -- instant health recovery (emergency heal)
- **Maribor Forest** -- adrenaline gain
- **White Honey** -- clears toxicity
