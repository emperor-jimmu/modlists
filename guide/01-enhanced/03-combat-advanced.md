# Advanced Combat

<span class="wave-badge wave-1">Wave 1</span>

## Enemy Weaknesses

### Necrophages
- **Weak to**: Fire (Igni), Vampire Oil
- **Dodge pattern**: Telegraphed swipes
- **Tips**: Yrden slows their movement

### Specters
- **Weak to**: Moon Dust bombs, Specter Oil
- **Dodge pattern**: Teleports, unseeable attacks
- **Tips**: Yrden makes them tangible

### Ogroids
- **Weak to**: Northern Wind bombs, Hanged Man's Venom
- **Dodge pattern**: Slow but powerful swings
- **Tips**: Aard interrupts their charges

### Beasts
- **Weak to**: Grapeshot, Beast Oil
- **Dodge pattern**: Lunges and bites
- **Tips**: Axii calms them temporarily

## Advanced Techniques

### Countering
- Tap parry just before enemy attack lands
- Staggers humanoid enemies for follow-up
- Does not work on most monsters

### Rend (Heavy Attack Skill)
- Hold heavy attack to charge
- Consumes stamina and adrenaline
- Armor-piercing, high damage

### Whirl (Light Attack Skill)
- Hold light attack to spin
- Hits multiple enemies
- Consumes stamina continuously
