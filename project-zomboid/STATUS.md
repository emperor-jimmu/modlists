## VERDICTS (2026-08-20)

Reviewed four Plysken Workshop mods (all explicit Build 42, all actively maintained — updates between 5 Aug and 20 Aug 2026). All four adopted; only one documented interaction with an existing mod.

### Adopted — Plysken Attachments Reborn (WS 3749848348) → Wave 1 (Items & Equipment)
- B42, SP & MP, 286 ratings (5★), updated 19 Aug 2026. B42 revival of Noir's Attachments (B41).
- Fits Wave 1's visual-upgrade pillar: weapons, tools, lights, and containers hang visibly on backpacks in 3D. Cosmetic + organizational; items stay in inventory.
- **Verify gate:** interacts with `[B42.20] Better Flashlights` (its belt-attach feature breaks while both are active). PAR author confirmed the fix is written for the next update (20 Aug 2026). Documented in mod-conflicts.md.

### Adopted — Plysken Irrigation Pipes (WS 3739173520) → Wave 1 (UI & QoL / Farming)
- B42, SP & MP, 205 ratings (5★), updated 5 Aug 2026.
- Auto-watering pipe network from rain collectors, with optional fertigation. Author-confirmed complementary to Wave 2's Water Pipes (Continued): crops vs house plumbing, no overlap.

### Adopted — Plysken Solar Revolution (WS 3725311427) → Wave 2 (Realism Systems)
- B42, SP & MP, 631 ratings (5★), updated 20 Aug 2026.
- Standalone B42 rewrite of Immersive Solar Arrays (B41) — no ISA dependency. Magazine-gated recipes, battery degradation, sandbox-tunable; not OP.
- Fits Wave 2's long-term base infrastructure alongside Immersive Blackouts and PlumbingPlus.

### Adopted — Plysken Underground Revolution (WS 3776279483) → Wave 2 (Building & Base)
- B42.20+, **single-player only** (no MP planned by author), 83 ratings (4★), updated 19 Aug 2026.
- Dig-your-own bunker: worksite bench, shovel passes, ladders, trapdoor. Engine caveat: the interior is borrowed from a nearby map cellar, so bunkers far from towns may not count as indoors — noted in the guide entry.
- SP-only accepted: every wave profile in this guide is solo-survival roleplay.
