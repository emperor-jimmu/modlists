# Creatures and Enemies

**MO2 Separator:** `NPCs - Enemies & Creatures`

All mods in this section belong to the `NPCs - Enemies & Creatures` MO2 separator.

> NPC-specific content → [NPCs](modlist-npcs.md).

## Enemy Variety

How hostile enemy groups feel less repetitive across ordinary play.

### Baseline
- **Lawless - A Bandit Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/88080)) — Cleanest vanilla-plus bandit variety. Bandits are the most overexposed enemy family.

### Alternatives
- **OBIS SE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/4145)) — Heavier legacy bandit route.
- **Haugbui - A Draugr Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/26188)) — Lighter undead route.
- **Skyrim Revamped - Complete Enemy Overhaul** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/14598)) — List-wide enemy rebalance.
- **Skyrim Immersive Creatures** — Out of scope; overlaps later creature subsections.

## Creature Visual Overhauls

Creature-specific visual direction layered on top of the Bellyaches texture foundation from → `Characters & Creatures`.

### Baseline
- **Fluffworks** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/56361)) — Broad furred-animal presentation layer.
- **Bears of the North** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/47541)) — Species standout for common large wildlife threat.
- **Iconic's Dragon Retexture** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/101712)) — Clean dragon presentation upgrade.
- **Dragons SE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/132218)) — Full dragon model replacer. Not compatible with Iconic's — choose one.
- **Draugrs - New models and textures** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/123170)) — Most-seen undead enemy.
- **Spider SE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/182638))
- **Deer Reworked - Ultimate** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/155433))
- **Cannibal Draugr on Solstheim** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/21238)) — Bloodmoon-flavored identity.
- **Hagraven - New models and Textures** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/160407))
- **Butterfly Improved by zzjay** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/37302))

### Alternatives

## Creature Behavior Improvements

How creatures attack, react, and express identity once combat starts.

### Baseline
- **Dragons Use Thu'um** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/87085)) — Smarter dragon shout usage and encounter AI.
- **The Restless Dead** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/94100)) — Undead-behavior baseline. Replaces abandoned Draugr Upgrades and Improvements.

### Alternatives
- **Epic Dragon Combat REDONE** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/126680)) — Full dragon combat overhaul: 20+ new dragon types, unique abilities, custom VFX. Self-contained — includes own models/textures so replaces baseline dragon visual direction.
- **Dragon War** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/51310)) — Heavier dragon route for fight structure/tempo.
- **SkyTEST Lite ESL** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/84539)) / **Animal Tweaks** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/1532)) / **Pelagius's Wildlife AI** ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/144909)) — Animal-side mods drifting into ecology territory.
