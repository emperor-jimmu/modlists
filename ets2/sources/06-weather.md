# Weather & Environment Mods

Overhauls ETS2 weather systems, seasonal environments, skyboxes, and vegetation. Grimes' seasonal mods form the backbone of this category, supplemented by standalone weather and vegetation improvements.

---

## Realistic Weather System V2.9 {⚠️ Standalone — cannot stack with any weather, graphics, rain, or Grimes seasonal mods}

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Version** | V2.9 |

| **Author** | Lionv8 |
| **Type** | Weather overhaul |
| **Downloads** | 130 |
| **Size** | 882.7 kB |

A lightweight weather-only mod that completely overhauls rain and thunderstorm behaviour:

- 10 realistic and unique types of rain and thunder intensities
- Realistic thunderstorm probability based on real climate data (2000-2025)
- More raindrops on windshield with realistic textures
- Rain probabilities that respect geographical locations
- Rain/thunder intensities for summer weather patterns in Europe
- Realistic road slipperiness and wind speed
- Realistic mist and car splash effects

**Compatibility:** 1.60 only. NOT compatible with other weather, graphics, rain, or Grimes seasonal mods.

**Performance Impact:** Negligible (<2% FPS loss) — small file size, light processing overhead.

**Pros:** Lightest weight weather mod, realistic rain/thunder based on real climate data, authentic road slipperiness
**Cons:** **Complete weather replacement** — cannot stack with ANY other weather, graphics, rain, or Grimes mods

- **Dynamic rain intensity** — storm cells ramp from light drizzle to heavy/extreme rainfall

- **Windshield droplets** — high-density drop physics with directional wind/rain interaction
- **Rain audio** — cabin EQ tuning, windshield rain positioning, exterior/world rain routing

### Notes

- **Grimes Seasons:** Only one season can be active at a time. A Seasons Pack ($2.99) bundles all five. Free downloads available individually via ShareMods with ads, or ad-free at $1 each.
- **Realistic Weather System V2.9** cannot be stacked with any other weather or graphics mod — it is a complete weather replacement.
- **Fael Environment** is not available for 1.60 (latest version v3.1 supports 1.37-1.40). It has been superseded by the mods listed above.
- **Improved Weather ETS2 Edition** V1.0 (by Ticreut29) is not updated for 1.60 — it targets 1.49.
